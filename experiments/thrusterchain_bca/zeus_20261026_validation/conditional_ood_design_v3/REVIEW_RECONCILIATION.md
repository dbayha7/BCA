## What action independence does and does not imply

Let `H(o)=1[g(o)>=0.4]` and let `p` be the behavior probability that the executed first command is inside the band. Because the current recorder action is independent of the current state, pointwise retention satisfies

`P(keep | o,t) = 1 - p*H(o)`.

Therefore the retained population state density, when it exists, is proportional to `p_original(o,t)*(1-p*H(o))`. It is **not globally unchanged**. In the idealized continuous-action law, `p=0.05`; with a continuous high-load share of 0.5, the retained high-load share is `0.95/(1+0.95)=19/39`, about 0.48718. Expected hole rows would be 76,800 times 0.025 = 1,920, but no corresponding realized count has been computed here. Exact executed-action/observed-load probabilities require the declared float32 rule.

Within fixed time/load-side strata, that retention probability is constant in the current state. Thus it is wrong to automatically call every residual state/normalization difference systematic action dependence. It is also wrong to assert exact balance of the fixed realized datasets. Conditional on an existing log and its realized retention quotas, random removal has expected retained sums equal to the quota-weighted original stratum sums; hole removal keeps its particular rows. Those sums need not agree. Temporal dependence, random quotas and condition-specific normalization require a reported balance check, not a blanket causal claim.

Source trajectory histories are shared by all row-subset conditions. They are not a differential intervention, but they still limit any claim that the retained dataset was generated by an avoidance policy. Reward and next-state labels depend on the removed action; their systematic changes are part of the intervention.

## Short glossary

- **OOD / ID:** outside / inside the specified data distribution or support definition; neither means bad / good action.
- **Q^pi:** discounted return after one candidate action and then the stated frozen policy.
- **MAE / RMSE:** mean absolute error / root mean squared error.
- **RMS distance:** square root of the mean squared coordinate differences; distinct from Euclidean norm by a dimension factor.
- **BC:** behavior-cloning term that penalizes deviation from logged actions.
- **Twin-min:** the minimum of the two native critic outputs; not the actor's Q1 operator.
- **U:** the original calibrated nonnegative local penalty scale, not a verified bound on Q^pi error.
- **BNP:** Bayesian nonparametric modeling; discussed as a source of design ideas, not added to BCA.
- **AUROC:** area under a binary-classification ROC curve; positive-class convention must be declared if used. It is not a primary outcome here.
