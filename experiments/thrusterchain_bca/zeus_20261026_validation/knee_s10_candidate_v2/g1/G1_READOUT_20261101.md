## What the record says about why (reported fields, then post hoc)

- **The fit is not converged at 20,000 updates, anywhere at the anchors, in the stall world.** Training MSE is still
  falling fast (13.1 at 5k, 2.3 at 20k, 0.84 at 60k). The anchor-value MSE is 21-28 at 20k and 8-10 at 60k. At 20k
  the contrast error KC is large at in-support slots too: median +0.78 to +0.87 at edge_700, +2.7 to +3.0 at
  mid_m40, +4.2 to +5.2 at zero, +6.5 to +7.3 at knee (a0 = +0.8). The departure at s10 (median KC +0.6 to +0.8 by the
  slot quantiles; 1.40-1.56 over valid anchors) is one symptom of a critic that is coarse everywhere at these states.
- **The smooth placebo fits.** In the no-stall world at 20k the same critic has anchor MSE 7-9 and median KC within
  +-0.05 at every slot, including s10 (smooth resolution share 0.55-0.64). The stall world's labels are the harder
  function: a kink in a0 at |a0| > 0.8 at high load, which 20,000 updates at the host's settings do not resolve.
- **More updates help but do not reach the bar (reported diagnostic, 60,000 updates).** Median KC(s10) 0.94 / 0.91 /
  1.10 (the median check passes in seeds 0 and 1), share 0.39 / 0.43 / 0.35 against 0.60, over-capture share
  0.53-0.64: the critic still values s10 too high relative to the knee edge at most anchors, with the truth in hand.
- **Direction of the error.** Every miss is over-capture (KC above the band), under-capture is 1-13%: the fitted
  critic smooths across the kink and over-values the past-knee action, in both worlds' sign convention. That is the
  same direction as the native TD critics showed on the knee-edge bed (exploratory Stage 1, both arms, KC_s05 about
  0.6-0.75), where labels did not remove it either.
- **24 of 96 anchors have rho(s10) <= 0** (s10 not worse under pi_ref in the stall world at that state); they are
  reported and excluded from the share by the rule's valid-anchor reading.

**Post hoc, not in the rule.** The in-support resolution the rule would have handed to the screen (band 1.84 at 20k,
about 1.2 at 60k) is far wider than the departure the screen's reads ask about (O1 bar 0.5, tol 0.15). Even had G1
passed on the median, the screen's reads would have been dominated by approximation error, not by withholding. G1 did
the job r4 gave it: it says a learning run on this host at this budget cannot be read as "withholding caused the
miss" or "BCA repaired it" at this slot.

## The cheapest robust next step (for the user's decision)

G1 costs about three CPU minutes per host configuration and needs no GPU and no training. Before spending 6-8 GPU
hours on any configuration, screen the host settings with G1 itself, under one small sealed rule written first:

- **Axis 1, update budget:** 20k (the host's), 40k, 60k, 100k. The 60k diagnostic shows the median passing in two
  seeds; the share needs more.
- **Axis 2, critic width:** 256 (the host's) and 512, at the same budgets.
- Everything else fixed: the same labels (computed once, 7.8 M exact steps, reusable), seeds, anchors, bars.
- **Reading:** the smallest configuration that passes G1 (every seed) becomes the host setting of candidate
  knee_s10_v2b, and the native screen re-seals with that setting (same package otherwise). If none passes, the slot
  s10 on this host is not learnable at these settings, and the candidate ledger records that as a bed-and-host
  finding.

The setting chosen this way is a host change, so the native screen's r4 statement "the pinned Unifloral TD3+BC with
its native settings" becomes "with its critic budget / width set to X, chosen by G1", disclosed in the rule.
