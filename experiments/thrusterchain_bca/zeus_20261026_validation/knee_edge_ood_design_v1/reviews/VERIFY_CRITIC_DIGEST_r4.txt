RESIDUAL:
- Even after the fixes, Stage 2 PROMISING is a weak screen. With three seeds and per-branch SD of about 3.55, a real +3 benefit passes only about 5-30% of the time, so NOT-PROMISING says little against BCA. A true null can still pass a few percent of the time, more if BCA beats the distance arm.
- R3 rests on an untested cross-world assumption: that BCA's general gain is about the same in the stall and no-stall worlds. If the two differ, R3 can wrongly pass or wrongly block, and no gate checks it.
- The noise level behind every operating characteristic (SD 3.55) comes from earlier proxy runs. Stage 2 reports its own check of that figure but does not act on it, so the stated pass rates could be off if the real noise is larger.
- Results at single checkpoints flipped sign across seeds in the prior study. Gating at 20k is correct, but sign instability can produce INCONCLUSIVE or mixed outcomes for reasons unrelated to the hypothesis.
- Stage 1's K1a reachability depends on rho under two different continuations. If CUTOFF's continuation gives a smaller predicted gap than RANDOM's, K1a can fail even when all the floors pass, which looks like a negative result but comes from the design.
- Step 0.3 can stop the plan early on the old critics (fold test or reach forecast). Those critics were trained on different data, so a stop there is informative only about them, and the onset-contrast warning is optimistically calibrated.
- GPU nondeterminism means reruns are not exact replicas. The rerun rules still leave some room to re-roll until a check passes, unless they are tightened.
- Inference is conditional on the forks that pass Stage 1, which were selected for showing damage. Using the replicate reduces that bias, but the forks themselves are still selected.
