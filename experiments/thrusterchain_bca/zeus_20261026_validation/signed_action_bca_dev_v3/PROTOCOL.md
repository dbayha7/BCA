# Expanded action-bank and signed-scale frozen diagnostic

One bounded exploratory run. Six saved 20k hole-condition checkpoints (native/BCA, three seeds) remain frozen. No actor, critic, target, or auxiliary optimizer update occurs; no policy-training study is authorized. This follows examined development results and is not independent confirmation of policy improvement.

## Frozen models, splits and controls

Restore the six v2 signed auxiliary parameter artifacts and the v1 freshly fitted symmetric Q1/twin-min auxiliary artifacts; verify each source hash, update count (2000), normalizer and complete checkpoint state. Recalibrate signed, Q1 and twin-min per checkpoint on the same 64 new behavior episodes and check each against 64 disjoint new behavior episodes, one randomly selected non-hole tuple per episode. Collection uses the original recorded-start behavior law, epsilon .25, and never reads the original protected transition records. One-step residual coverage is descriptive on this selected behavior distribution only; it does not guarantee true-Q, selected-action, or shifted-state coverage. Signed calibration uses 1000 Bayesian bootstrap draws, alpha .1, beta .95, alongside its split-quantile control.

On the original 768 designated retained training probes evaluate each frozen actor. Reference episodes/neighbor statistics for compatibility are excluded by outer fold; bandwidth and radius are chosen by nested inner folds. Auxiliary models themselves were fitted using the full retained training set, so their probe predictions are in-sample, not cross-fitted. Signed scale controls are fixed using only these training probes after the fresh calibration and before any Q-pi. Match signed mean magnitude to mean 3*Q1 width; separately match signed action-gradient RMS to 3*Q1 width action-gradient RMS. Match eta for guarded compatibility action-gradient RMS to 25% of Q1 action-gradient RMS. An additional symmetric width-only control matches the full guarded hybrid action-gradient RMS. Require finite positive denominators >1e-6 and all coefficients in (0,30]; otherwise stop, never clip or tune. Multiplying signed B creates an experimental score, not the original calibrated bound. Record all numerators, denominators, and coefficients. The guard-off cost uses the exact same eta as the guard-on cost, making its gradient strength potentially different and explicitly risky; it is diagnostic only.

Scores on identical actions include raw Q1, fresh-calibrated symmetric Q1 and twin-min, unscaled signed Bayesian and split thresholds, mean-matched signed, gradient-matched signed, revised compatibility alone, signed+guarded compatibility, symmetric Q1 + guarded compatibility, symmetric Q1 + unguarded compatibility, and a symmetric-width control matched to guarded hybrid gradient. Score = Q1 - specified penalty. No score is a new trained actor. No evaluation Q-pi participates in matching, calibration, candidate generation, state selection or control selection.

## Fresh pools and candidate selection

Each context has 64 new reset families: 16 each at t=0,25,50,75. Uniform random prefix actions and initial states are generated once and shared across all checkpoints. Ordinary reset seeds 2026122000..2026122063, strong 2026122100..2026122163; prefix PCG64 seeds 2026122200/2026122201. Fresh behavior root seed 2026122300 and tuple-selection seed 2026122301; the collector authenticates original and previous seed vectors and checks all stream collisions before simulation.

For each checkpoint/state form 15 candidates in this exact order: actor, negative and positive .2 perturbation to each of its four coordinates, clipped to [-1,1], nearest deterministically tie-broken retained logged action from the state/time reference, and five historical fixed vectors (-.5,0,0,0), (-.25,0,0,0), (0,0,0,0), (.25,0,0,0), (.5,0,0,0). Keep and account for clipped duplicate actions. The nearest logged action is a comparator, not a claim of support or optimality.

Within each 16-state time block the first four are the natural stream. For each of the remaining 12, calculate raw-Q1 top-two gaps over distinct full action vectors, normalized by (1+absolute top Q1); take the median across the six frozen checkpoints. Choose four with the smallest median, breaking ties by pool index. This gives 16 natural and 16 ambiguity-selected states per context. Selection is shared across checkpoints, requires no simulator returns, and defines a conditional population that must not be extrapolated to natural starts. Persist all pool actions, raw-Q1 scores, selection indices, state/time and duplicate counts before any Q-pi evaluation. Other fixed scores are computed from frozen models without Q-pi input. Each Q-pi is the selected first action followed by that checkpoint's own frozen deterministic actor, gamma .98, finite horizon 100, no terminal bootstrap.

## Metrics, screen and work limits

Predeclare natural stream as primary and ambiguity stream as a separately reported conditional mechanism test. For each seed, arm, context and stratum report all-state and high-load mean regret to best of the 15 candidates, changed/improved/harmed/tied decisions versus raw Q1 using return tolerance .1, per-row signed Q errors and MAE including existing fixed-band strata, and actor-versus-coordinate-perturbation ranking accuracy on pairs with absolute true Q-pi gap >.1 (exclude exact duplicates; near-ties excluded). The eight perturbation pairs and 15 candidates share states; three paired training seeds, not action rows, are the independent trained-replication units.

A cell has an informative gap if at least one nonduplicate actor-versus-perturbation pair differs in Q-pi by >.1. An improvement-screen cell requires at least eight such state families and at least one raw-Q1 bank mistake with regret >.1; if either fails, the improvement decision is inconclusive, though all descriptive metrics are reported. On natural states a qualifying cell requires guarded hybrid mean regret at least .05 below both raw Q1 and gradient-matched hybrid-width, no higher than symmetric Q1, and harmful switches <= improvements (all with .1 action tolerance). A context passes only if >=2/3 seeds qualify in each checkpoint group. If insufficient eligible cells prevent that possibility, status is inconclusive; otherwise absent passing qualifications status is failed. Enriched screen uses identical criteria and is separate. These are exploratory engineering thresholds, not a significance test or evidence of policy-return gain.

Simulator budget exact if completed: 4,800 pool-prefix + 104 simulator-parity + 12,800 fresh behavior + 360,000 forced-continuation transitions = **377,704**. There are exactly 5,760 forced Q-pi references (32 states x 15 actions x six checkpoints x two contexts), 257 resets (128 pool, 128 behavior, one parity), 18 calibration refreshes, zero free-policy episodes and zero actor/critic/target/auxiliary updates. Pool critic and actor inference, training-probe matching and candidate scoring are separately accounted; cap and no changes to existing source/results/checkpoints. Two-hour deadline, fail closed with no automatic retry; preserve incomplete ledger and partial data rather than claim completion.
