# Paired Q1-width refits and capped-width frozen-controller evaluation

This is one bounded exploratory follow-up to the v4 common-action diagnostic. Six final 20k hole-condition checkpoints, their actors, online and target critics, normalization, and targets stay frozen. No actor, critic, target, or policy optimizer update is authorized. The old v3 calibration data, v4 outcomes and all preceding diagnostics have already been inspected and are development evidence, not independent confirmation.

## Questions and prespecified controls

1. How much do symmetric-width action contrasts vary when separately trained auxiliary networks share stochastic inputs? For each of three paired training seeds, fit four paired native/BCA Q1-width auxiliaries. Corresponding paired fits have the same initialization seed, PCG64 minibatch indices, per-step JAX keys, target-noise and bootstrap streams; the frozen actors and critics and their corresponding TD targets remain checkpoint-specific. For training seed `s` and replicate `r=0..3`, use initialization/data key `2027011000 + 10*s + r` and calibration key `2027015000 + 10*s + r`. Each fit uses exactly 2,000 updates and batch size 256, for at most 24 fits and 48,000 auxiliary updates. Reuse the 64 v3 calibration episodes, with paired calibration randomness, for at most 24 refreshes. Evaluate each fitted width on the previously inspected v4 shared banks: at most 23,040 width rows. Report width-gap distributions, between-pair difference, within-checkpoint variation, and all replicate values; four refits cannot establish causal effects. No simulator transitions are authorized in this component. Existing auxiliary models and these refits are development-used; do not choose the fresh-evaluation reranker, cap, or checkpoints using their outcomes.

2. Can limiting the action-dependent symmetric-width contrast improve frozen-controller outcomes? For the frozen actor action `a_pi`, use `P(s,a)=clip(3*(U(s,a)-U(s,a_pi)), -cap_t, cap_t)` and `score=Q1-P`. The unclipped center alone cannot change action ranking. The clipped score has no original calibration or safety guarantee. Reconstruct the existing v3 auxiliaries and references deterministically using their archived calibration rows and keys. Compare five fixed controllers: original actor, actor-centered-bank raw Q1, original symmetric Q1 minus three times width, the capped-contrast score, and the original guarded compatibility hybrid. Compute compatibility and coefficients exactly as in v3. No parameter fitting or simulator outcome may choose a controller or change its score.

The action bank has 15 bounded slots: actor action; eight +/-0.2 single-coordinate perturbations; the existing nearest logged action; and five previously fixed actions. Retain duplicates for score/controller comparisons and use deterministic first-slot tie-breaking. For cap estimation only, deduplicate full action vectors before taking top-two raw-Q1 values. For each checkpoint and time bin 0-24, 25-49, 50-74, 75-99, set the cap to the median of finite strictly positive top-two gaps on the 768 designated retained training probes. Require at least 50 distinct episodes with valid gaps per bin and checkpoint or stop inconclusive. These probes were used in fitting and are not independent validation. Persist and hash caps before creating or inspecting fresh simulator outcomes.

## Fresh evaluation and estimands

Use 16 new initial reset seeds per context: ordinary 2026130000 through 2026130015 and strong current 2026130100 through 2026130115. Audit these against original logged/evaluation seeds and v2-v4 consumed behavior, reset and prefix streams before use. Within each context, reuse the same initial physical states for every checkpoint and controller; report their dependence. Use the packaged `core_v4` reference, its recorded and strong-current start laws, gamma 0.98, 100-step horizon and the original action/observation conventions. Verify the existing 104-step simulator parity before fresh evaluation.

Run each fixed controller for a complete episode; its selected actions change its subsequent states, so this is online evaluation of a fixed controller, not policy training or strict-offline policy selection. Store each discounted return and relevant action/score/guard/cap telemetry. Separately, at each fresh initial state evaluate each of the five fixed controllers' first actions, followed by the corresponding original frozen actor, for the same finite horizon and discount. Those 960 references estimate first-action Q-pi for the saved continuation, not Q-star or the full reranker's return. Select first actions without reading any fresh Q-pi or returns. Report actual references separately from complete-episode returns.

The primary comparison is paired, per-checkpoint-seed mean discounted full-episode return: capped versus raw and versus symmetric, separately by context and native/BCA checkpoint group. The prespecified engineering screen requires at least two of three seeds in each group to improve by at least +1 return unit against both controls under strong current, and no seed in either group to lose more than 1 mean return unit against its original actor under ordinary current. Missing cases, insufficient valid caps, failure to complete the budget, or nonfinite/invalid outputs are inconclusive rather than success. Report per-seed effects, all paired episode values, harmful and improved first-action changes, cap saturation and guard activation; no statistical-significance or population guarantee is implied by three seeds. Strong-current first-action and original-actor comparisons remain visible even if the primary screen fails.

## Budget, provenance and stop rules

At most 96,000 full-controller transitions plus 96,000 first-action-reference transitions plus 104 parity transitions: **192,104 simulator transitions**. At most 960 full episodes, 960 first-action references and 1,152,000 reranker candidate-score rows, plus separately accounted retained-training-probe/cap and auxiliary-bank evaluations. Use 32 fresh physical starts, shared by copying the state; record all resets and any other charged operations. Log early termination and actual work. Record deterministic v3 reconstruction refreshes separately from 24 paired-refit refreshes. Preserve complete source, input, checkpoint, cap and result hashes, fresh seed audit, counter totals, software/backend settings, wall time, and pre/post frozen checkpoint digests. Require finite actions within environment bounds and fail closed on accounting, numerical, source-identity, or simulator-parity errors.

The source and cap seal must exist before fresh starts. Do not automatically retry, tune, replace failed or missing observations, expand the seed set, alter the predeclared screen, resume an incomplete run without a new authorized attempt, or start policy training. Preserve partial results and accounting if interrupted. Use a 7,200-second hard maximum for each bounded component and report actual times; the experiment's maximum is therefore 14,400 seconds across the two components.
