# Central-quartile matched training study — prepared, execution disabled

This is a development study specification for review. The environment audit and passed central-quartile design gate justify this construction for the declared synthetic task; they do not establish BCA efficacy or validate the new training integration. No production run is authorized by preparing this package. The expanded budget and target-runtime checks must be reviewed before execution is enabled in a separately recorded authorization change.

## Question and scope

Does original direct-Q BCA improve learned-policy return and the accuracy of its pessimistic score relative to the policy it actually executes, when logged action labels are removed conditionally on state? Does improvement exceed a simple penalty matched on training-data action gradients? Compare the hole with a state/count-matched random-removal control; the full-data arm contextualizes overall loss of information. This is not Q-star estimation, an isolated action-only causal experiment, or a Bayesian-specific ablation.

The immutable environment audit is `../environment_audit_v1/AUDIT.md`. The design gate report SHA256 is `91833bd4acf5a7657b2d43751c3800ffbe7b6b60c4c7c2573202b88faa522743`. The original failed negative-band gate remains a failed development screen. All existing source data, calibration episodes and design results are development-consumed. New evaluation seeds provide independent simulator starts conditional on this selected design, not independent benchmark discovery or a locked confirmation claim.

## Data, preprocessing and exposure

Use only the original 768 fitting episodes and 128 calibration episodes, never the other 384 episodes. Original logged tuples are selected intact. The hole is `abs(a[0]) <= 0.25` and `obs[10] + obs[11] >= 0.4`, where stored float32 coordinates are promoted to float64 before arithmetic. Full replay has 76,800 rows. Hole and random replay each have 67,108 rows; 9,692 labels are removed. Random removal matches hole retention exactly within source episode × ten-step time bin × high/low observed-load side, using the already frozen gate seed. This matches counts and coarse state/time strata, not complete states, reward or successor distributions. In expectation conditional retention changes state density; surviving states may reflect earlier removed actions. This is tuple-label withholding, not collection without historical exposure.

Fit common population observation mean/std only on the intersection of rows retained by both hole and random controls. Supply the same raw-observation initialization fixture to both native and auxiliary initializers. Optimizers subsequently see only the selected condition's replay. No deleted-row observations, calibration rows or evaluation starts enter normalization. The native normalization epsilon remains 0.001. Common normalization intentionally withholds some full-data statistics to isolate preprocessing across conditions; this is disclosed, not called default per-condition preprocessing.

All conditions use the same 128 calibration rows, one per original calibration episode, selected from hole-eligible rows by the existing outcome-independent replacement rule (13 original rows replaced). No episode is silently dropped/backfilled. Empty eligible episodes stop the study. Calibration reuses development episodes at 10k and 15k. Their thresholds influence later BCA actors and critics, so adaptive reuse defeats a fresh split-conformal interpretation. Calibration is on retained support and does not establish conditional hole coverage, selected-action coverage, shifted-policy validity, or true-Q coverage. The posterior remains a calibration-risk object.

## Models, seeds and fork

Use the pinned Titan UniFloral source revision `f2dc1278eae18ed3e22c92255119c54885414d17`, not CORL, with twin critics and the existing auxiliary architecture. Three independent initialization seeds are paired across the three data conditions; exact seeds are in `protocol.json`. Every condition starts from fresh network and optimizer initialization. No model checkpoint previously exposed to hole labels is used for warmup. Fresh initial weights and shared normalization must match across conditions within a seed and differ between seeds.

Each of nine cells performs a 10,000-critic-update native warmup with shadow auxiliary fitting and calibration at 10k. Fork its complete state into three 10,000-update branches: native κ=0; original BCA κ=3; frozen-fork actor-distance penalty. Save and restore online/target parameters, all optimizer states and steps, PRNG keys, phase and held minibatch, auxiliary state/residual units, posterior, actor reference and refresh counters. The original candidate microsteps reuse each 256-row batch for two critic updates and one actor/target update. Matching branch PRNG streams and replay mappings are shared across arms within a cell. Data conditions have different replay sizes/mappings, so equal PRNG draws are not identical tuples across conditions.

All arms preserve native Q1 actor loss, stopped Q-normalization coefficient, BC term, smoothed twin-min target, discount 0.98, 100-step finite horizon, and terminal bootstrap zero. BCA adds its live-action-gradient penalty to the actor objective; it does not directly rewrite critic predictions or TD targets. Shadow auxiliary fitting and recalibration at 15k are applied to all branches for matched auxiliary work. Shadow work has no gradient path to native/distance actors. Native inference requires no uncertainty model. This compute-matched baseline is more expensive than ordinary native TD3+BC; report learner and shadow cost separately.

At 10k, match `κ_distance * ∇a D` to `3 * ∇a U` in RMS norm on one retained logged fitting transition per training episode (768 rows), separately per cell. `D=19.29619572569095*mean((a-pi_10k(s))^2)`. Matching is immutable thereafter, uses no return/evaluation labels and has no clipping/fallback. Norm <=1e-6, nonfinite/unrepresentable coefficient, or invalid calibration stops the attempt. The distance gradient is exactly zero at its anchor actor at the fork: this is only a logged-action gradient-strength control, not matching the actual initial actor gradient or matching gradient direction. It does not isolate the Bayesian contribution.

## Fixed evaluation and estimands

Train all cells before evaluating any checkpoint. No checkpoint selection or outcome-driven extension is permitted. Save untrained 0, common 10k, and each branch at 15k and 20k. Evaluate free policies for all 72 snapshots and Q references for the 63 trained snapshots. For each snapshot use 32 fresh, shared starts in each of ordinary and strong-current contexts. Strong current changes observed initial current from the recorded [0.1,0.7] law to [0.7,1.1] with unchanged dynamics; report it separately, never as hidden-dynamics shift.

Use a separate set of 32 common Q anchors per context. Generate them once using the original reset distribution and outcome-independent uniform action prefixes, with eight anchors at each t=0,25,50,75. Preserve float64 physical state and exact time. Do not reset interior anchors to t=0. Check the existing reference adapter against actual environment steps at t=0,25,50,99. Each candidate gets the full discounted return of its first action followed by that model's own deterministic frozen actor through step 100. This is Q-pi, not the smoothed lagged twin-min TD target. Stochastic target noise is a training operator; physical continuations here are deterministic given state/action/policy.

Each anchor has six candidates: its own actor action (slot 0, reported separately) and five fixed shared actions with a0=[−0.5,−0.25,0,0.25,0.5] and other three coordinates zero. The fixed actions are common across models, unlike an actor-centered bank; zero is the recorder's action median. This does not establish dense joint action support. Candidate selection ties within 1e-6 choose the first slot. Report all candidates, not only model errors or useful BCA cases. Free rollouts and forced one-action interventions are distinct.

For each trained snapshot report raw Q1, raw twin-min, BCA score Q1−3U when valid, and frozen-anchor distance score when applicable. Compare raw and adjusted scores on the same critic/reference, and compare native-trained/BCA-trained critics separately. Across trained policies Q-pi itself differs, so a difference in errors is not the same-Q-function treatment effect. Report ordinary/strong-current and high-load band/retained flank/low-load band separately. Overestimation is predicted score minus Q-pi >0.1, underestimation <−0.1, near-zero otherwise. Report all counts/denominators, signed magnitudes, MAE/RMSE, paired improvement/worsening/near-tie in absolute error (0.1 tolerance), and sign crossings. Support novelty labels use the fixed mask, independently of error sign. Positive or negative OOD actions are both retained in analysis.

Report free discounted/undiscounted return, exposure to the withheld condition, score-selected candidate gain versus own actor, oracle-bank regret and harmful switches (>0.1 return loss). Five fixed actions allow common action comparisons; oracle-bank regret is privileged diagnostic headroom, not a deployable method. Learning curves at 0/10k/15k/20k are fixed resolution, not the exact time-to-adjust.

## Decision rules and independent units

Primary learned-policy contrast: ordinary-context final BCA minus native return in hole data, paired by initialization seed and episode. A promising policy screen requires at least +1 discounted-return unit for each of the three training-seed means, and BCA minus distance >=0 for every seed. One unit is a prespecified practical screen in this dimensionless task (ten times the error tolerance), not a physical performance requirement or validated universal threshold. Also report every result if this screen fails; never tune κ, masks or criteria after viewing it.

Accuracy is a separate declared objective: report final high-load fixed-band MAE improvement of BCA's adjusted score over native Q1 for each seed, and same-critic raw-to-adjusted improvement/harm. A promising accuracy screen requires >=1 unit in all three seeds. Failure does not negate a measured policy benefit or justify suppressing it. A missing-label-specific benefit additionally requires a positive hole-minus-random interaction in each seed; full-data comparisons and low-load retained-band controls contextualize this interaction. Cross-load comparisons alone do not isolate label removal because load changes dynamics.

Learning adequacy: full-data native final ordinary return must exceed its own untrained reference by >=1 unit in each seed. Otherwise declare insufficient baseline learning for efficacy attribution, without increasing training. If fewer than eight high-load Q anchor families in a context are available, conditional accuracy estimates are descriptive/inconclusive; no resampling. Numerical invalidity, source mismatch, unsafe calibration, budget exhaustion, missing job or unknown counts stops the study as incomplete. Mixed seed effects fail the stated exploratory screen, not prove equivalence. There is no automatic successor experiment.

Training seeds (n=3) are the independent units for algorithm variability. The same environment starts are paired across policies and reused across checkpoints; rows, candidates, times and checkpoints are not additional independent training replications. Always show three seed effects. Pooled point estimates average seeds equally; descriptive intervals use whole seed blocks and whole start families, with the same resampled starts across arms. With only three seeds such intervals are coarse and cannot establish reliable population superiority. No significance claim or post-hoc subgroup promotion is allowed.

## Resources, recovery and hold

Nine 10k warmups + twenty-seven 10k branches = 360,000 critic, 180,000 actor/actor-target/critic-target, 360,000 auxiliary optimizer updates, 36 calibrations and 36,000 posterior draws. Replay draws: 46,080,000 (each reused twice); critic and auxiliary row uses are each 92,160,000. Gradient matching uses 6,912 retained logged rows with no updates. Count real dispatch attempts and committed updates separately.

Evaluation: 4,608 free episodes / 460,800 steps; 24,192 candidate Q references / 1,512,000 continuation steps; 2,400 shared random-prefix steps; 104 parity steps. Total maximum 1,975,304 simulator transitions. No simulator labels enter training/calibration/matching. Caps are prospective and fail closed.

Proposed global ceiling: 43,200 seconds (12 hours), with each training job <=1,800 seconds and evaluation <=7,200 seconds, all bounded by remaining global time. This is a ceiling, not an estimate/promise. Saved Zeus 10k warmups took 635.80 and 704.52 seconds; prior 2k native/BCA/distance jobs took 139.78–149.20 seconds. Linear extrapolation yields roughly 6.4–7.5 hours for training; larger replay, compilation, shadow calibration and immutable checkpoint I/O may change this. The previous design gate took 48.41 seconds for 28,104 steps; linear extrapolation of the enlarged evaluator is roughly 0.95 hours and is unvalidated. Allow up to two hours evaluation and remaining headroom under the global cap. No paid compute or allocator/batch-size changes.

Every 1,000 critic updates save an immutable complete checkpoint with hashes and counts, and fixed 10k/15k/20k snapshots. On interruption preserve all receipts and the last durable checkpoint. An in-flight update or gap after the last journal leaves actual consumed work uncertain; do not treat saved counts as exact total dispatches. No automatic restart or resume. A continuation would require a separately reviewed identity, remaining budgets and accounting; this package does not authorize it. Atomic one-shot reservation and scoped overlap checks remain required even after approval.

Target verification still required: exact installed GPU packages/devices, actual model initialization/normalization, tiny synthetic native parity/full-state restore/live-gradient tests, and full Gymnasium checker. CPU fixtures cannot substitute for those checks. The intended target validation is capped at 900 seconds, uses synthetic data only and no pretrained model, and does not reserve the production attempt. Budget approval is separate from validation.

## Adversarial-review additions before any outcome

These additions refine the declared measurements without changing the environment, mask, training arms, seeds, optimizer budgets or simulator-transition budget. They are part of the proposed specification, not claims established by the passed gate.

**Behavior cloning is a competing mechanism.** The recorder's action law is symmetric. Removing the central first-action quartile leaves symmetric tails with conditional population mean zero; finite data need not have exactly zero mean. Squared BC is minimized at that conditional mean and can therefore pull the actor into the hole. Exposure is not proof of critic-driven exploitation. At the 10k fork and each 15k/20k branch snapshot, a fixed training-only diagnostic separates Q, BC and penalty actor-gradient contributions, and first-action gradient directions on the 768 retained logged probes. It reports exact stopped raw-Q normalization, actor-parameter gradient norms/cosines and action-gradient directions. There are 63 diagnostics / 48,384 probe rows, no optimizer changes, no simulator labels, and no coefficient retuning. These are local gradient diagnostics, not causal ablations of the full learning trajectory. Native's hypothetical κ=3 uncertainty contribution, where reported, must be labeled hypothetical; its actual actor penalty remains zero.

**Sign matters.** For each common fixed bank report `Qpi(center) - max(Qpi(left flank), Qpi(right flank))`, its positive/negative/near-zero counts at tolerance 0.1, the fraction whose best fixed-bank action is inside the hole, and corresponding raw/adjusted score margins. Report selected-action gains, regret and harmful switches regardless of sign. An action may be both unsupported and good. A large absolute center/flank gap does not mean the hole action is bad or that lowering its score helps. The earlier gate's gaps used the original actor's other three commands and that actor's continuation; this proposed common complete-action bank instead uses zero for the other commands. Continuation policies may change gap magnitude and sign. Neither bank's result is silently transferred to the other.

**Operational and fixed-reference value panels are distinct.** The primary accuracy panel remains each model's own-policy Q-pi. A secondary common-reference panel reuses the already-computed full-data native 20k policy's five fixed-action values, separately per initialization seed and context, for every model/checkpoint on exactly the same states, times and complete actions. This adds no rollouts. It measures score deviation against one fixed reference policy, not whether every critic correctly estimates the policy it was trained to evaluate. Own-actor slots are excluded from that panel because their first actions differ. No reference is selected by its return.

**Join measurements on visited states without new simulations.** Retain each free trajectory's states, actions and rewards. At t=0,25,50,75, compute exact discounted remaining return for its recorded actor action from the cached reward suffix, and score that same action with Q1/twin-min and Q1−3U. This yields 16,128 joined rows across trained snapshots: error, current/time, action and the fixed withholding-mask label belong to the same visited state. These rows add 16,128 critic and width queries but zero simulator steps or actor queries. The mask labels constructed novelty relative to retained labels. It does not prove empirical local state density or joint four-dimensional action support at those newly visited states; those properties remain unknown. Do not infer that join from separate gate aggregates or call forced anchors policy-visited data.

**Rescue requires demonstrated damage.** In addition to BCA-minus-control and hole-minus-random interactions, report native hole-minus-random return and native hole-minus-random own-policy MAE. A policy-rescue screen requires native hole minus random mean return <=−1 in every seed, the original promising BCA policy screen, and positive BCA-specific hole-minus-random return interaction in every seed. An accuracy-rescue screen separately requires native hole minus random MAE >=1 in every seed, the original BCA accuracy screen, and positive accuracy interaction in every seed. Otherwise report improvement, stability or harm in its actual context; do not describe a generic BCA gain as repairing damage caused by withholding. These remain exploratory screens with only three independent initializations and one fixed dataset/removal mask.

**Support evidence remains bounded.** The passed gate used 13 standardized physical coordinates, with time handled by ten-step strata and high/low observed-load strata. It excluded the complete query episode when constructing neighbors, including leave-one-reference-episode-out training reference distances. Its higher 95% quantile was estimated from only 32 distinct-episode reference anchors in each stratum. That threshold has finite-sample uncertainty and was a relative typicalness rule, not a calibrated density boundary. It checked marginal first-action occupancy in fixed 64-neighbor sets, not joint four-action support. The state's high-load fraction is one half in the symmetric population, not necessarily exactly one half of a finite replay. The training study retains these limitations and does not relabel the gate as full support validation.

The revised evaluation query totals are 40,320 critic rows (each returns both heads) and 40,320 width rows, including the visited-state join. The ordinary rollout/forced-Q simulator total stays 1,975,304. Frozen-distance scoring adds its actual anchor-actor rows to the separately bounded actor ledger. Gradient diagnostics and matching are additional inference/autodiff work and are not hidden inside the optimizer count. All inference, compilation, diagnostics, checkpoint I/O and evaluation count against the proposed 43,200-second total. Every 1k state plus final duplicate exports is about 405 full-state files at the existing approximately 10.35 MB size: roughly 4.2 GB before actor exports/logs/receipts; require at least 6 GiB free workspace capacity before a production reservation. No network downloads are part of execution.

The remaining material review choices are the 12-hour/approximately 4–6 GB resource allowance and acceptance of this limited mechanism study: three initialization seeds on one fixed dataset/mask, logged-action strength matching, adaptive retained-support calibration, own-policy operational errors plus the declared fixed-reference panel, and mask-only novelty on newly visited states. No extra common-reference rollout matrix, density estimator, Bayesian ablation, head sweep or benchmark redesign is implied.
