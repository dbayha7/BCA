# Audit of the five-component conditional OOD draft

Date: 2026-10-27. Audited input: `../conditional_ood_design_v1/DRAFT_PROTOCOL.md`, SHA-256 `7bebb74cb4949cb48520e548dbb98e5aa168cdfaf57f3d142b7e783675f3b5ce`. The original draft is preserved. This audit and the corrected v2 are documents only: no dataset changes, model execution, simulator calls, installs or protected-record reads.

**Verdict:** retain the five components, but revise the operational support check, causal claims and learning-time specification. They cover the advisor's main accuracy/decision/return questions in principle. They are not yet a ready experiment: conditional coverage, exact budgets, practical criteria and missing Titan evidence remain unresolved. The proposed controls do not identify a Bayesian-specific benefit or adaptation after observing a shifted environment.

## Findings that change the design

1. **The region has a mechanical rationale, not a validated value opportunity.** The source thrust equation supports the negative stall-knee location and opposing positive load. It does not establish useful Q^pi gaps, baseline mistakes or BCA benefit. No new pre-removal conditional counts have been computed. Keep one proposed region and require a geometry-only coverage audit. Separately interpret value-gap exposure using the already planned native-policy reference values under a predeclared, BCA-independent criterion; do not add a new diagnostic rollout stage or search regions using outcomes.
2. **The full four-action support check would almost always be empty.** For uniform actions on `[-1,1]^4`, an interior L-infinity ball of radius 0.05 occupies fraction `(0.1/2)^4 = 0.00000625`. Its expected count among 64 state neighbors is only **0.0004**, even before withholding. This is analytic design arithmetic, not a measured result. Replace that gate with counts in the intervention's first-coordinate bands inside fixed state neighborhoods. Keep full-action distances as sparsity diagnostics; marginal band counts do not prove support for every four-action vector.
3. **Matching controls constrain only their declared quantities.** Episode/time/load-stratified matching equalizes row counts and coarse occupancy, not exact continuous states, rewards, successors or condition-specific normalization. The effect is a conditional-label-removal pipeline effect. Reward/successor changes may be consequences of the action intervention; matching them away would change the question. Report them and residual imbalance rather than asserting they are controlled.
4. **Fixed-data curves are not online adaptation.** They can measure offline learning progress and generalization to ordinary/strong currents. Define an optional first-scheduled-checkpoint threshold crossing only if its accuracy/return criterion is fixed before labels. Explicitly identify the unpenalized warmup and BCA activation; add one proposed 11k checkpoint for early post-activation resolution. Do not infer exact convergence time or adaptation after observing new current data.
5. **The uncertainty target differs from the requested value error.** Original BCA uses a twin-min critic against a smoothed, lagged native TD target. The actor uses current Q1, and the evaluator follows the deterministic current actor. Preserve all three definitions. BCA leaves the critic target formula unchanged, but its changed actor can change the numerical targets later. Neither a calibrated residual nor lower positive error establishes better Q^pi accuracy.
6. **Defer optional expansion.** Multiple hole widths, half-data and changed kick covariance do not directly answer the first comparison. Keep one hole width, the ordinary/strong-current factor, native/BCA and the previously defined simple control. No Bayesian ablation, BNP model, additional host or head-count sweep is added.

## Leakage and exposure audit

- **Forbidden labeled exposure:** excluded `(s,a,r,s')` rows entering critic replay, actor BC, auxiliary fitting, calibration, matching, model selection, or a broad-trained initialization. Record source row/episode IDs at every consumer. Never use simulator probe values to fit uncertainty or tune coefficients.
- **Normalization:** learner statistics use retained training current observations only. A common audit transform may use original training states but must remain outside training, coefficient matching, calibration and model selection. It is diagnostic design information, not evidence of zero access to every original observation.
- **Target and actor queries:** predicting Q or U at a hole action is permitted. A model's unlabeled query is not a logged held-out transition label. Forbidding it would alter native TD3+BC and prevent studying extrapolation.
- **Trajectory histories and successors:** row filtering cannot undo causal effects of earlier removed actions. Valid retained successors stay attached to their source tuples. The claim is missing conditional logged labels, not training on avoidance-policy trajectories.
- **Calibration:** use the separate eligible calibration episodes and original mechanism. Eligibility is geometry-only. The same bank reused at declared refreshes does not establish fresh exchangeability or simultaneous coverage across checkpoints; OOD and selected-action guarantees are not inferred.
- **Selection:** all new tests are reporting-only after the specification freezes. Readiness and geometry checks cannot be a hidden loop over BCA outcomes. Old protected selection/final-calibration outcomes remain sealed. Invalid/refused cases remain in accounting.

An independent reviewer challenged identifiability and scope and agreed with the six changes above. The corrected draft is `DRAFT_PROTOCOL.md` in this v2 directory. It remains **not executable**. Next work is a separately authorized training-geometry/role audit and protocol freeze, not a run or method revision.

## Relevant external safeguards, without added methods

These references were supplied by the preceding primary-source review; this audit adds no downloads or external experiment work.

- [OpenOOD](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.09301v5) motivates separate validation/final testing and near/far reporting. Here severity is frozen physical geometry or training-only support distance; image semantic classes or detector difficulty are not transferred as RL truth labels.
- [NINCO](https://github.com/j-cb/NINCO) motivates careful contamination auditing. Our independent inside/outside/boundary mask fixtures are a design adaptation of that concern, not a claim that the image benchmark implements conditional-action masks.
- [Nalisnick et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09136) cautions against treating likelihood as OOD ground truth. The known withholding mask plus pre/post occupancy is primary construction evidence here.
- [Lyapunov Density Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10524) distinguishes present support from future trajectory support. A forced first action or a supported starting action cannot replace whole-policy rollout exposure accounting.
- [BCQ](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/fujimoto19a.html) motivates recording unsupported actor/target queries and their bootstrapping context. It does not justify assuming every OOD action is overestimated or inferior.

Maintain separate truth columns for **support novelty** and **overestimation relative to Q^pi**. Withheld actions can be better, worse or equivalent. None of these safeguards adds a density model, detector or new penalty to BCA.
