# Independent review of the conditional OOD design (v1 draft, v2 audit and v2 draft)

Date: 27 October 2026. Reviewed on Zeus at checkout `50099436`. Inputs: `../conditional_ood_design_v1/DRAFT_PROTOCOL.md`
(SHA-256 `7bebb74c…`), this folder's `AUDIT.md`, `AUDIT_RECEIPT.json` and `DRAFT_PROTOCOL.md` (v2), and the source files they
cite. All 15 hashes in the v1 source receipt and the dataset hash recompute exactly.

This is a document review only. No training, simulation, calibration, dataset decoding or protected-split reads took
place. Every count below marked **analytic** is a closed-form expectation from the documented recorder and start law,
not a measurement. The review ran as two multi-agent passes, each finding checked by a separate refuting agent: five
lenses on v1 (run `wf_1bc8b803-dad`) and a claim-by-claim check of the v2 audit plus three gap searches on v2 (run
`wf_943039b2-a3b`). Only findings that survived verification are reported, at their verified severity.

Terms used here: **OOD** is out of distribution. **Q^pi** is the discounted return of taking one command and then
following a given policy. **Hole** is the withheld region `g >= 0.4` and `|a0 + k| <= 0.05`, where `g` is current plus
first wave coordinate, `a0` is the first thruster's command and `k = 0.800000011920929` is the stall knee. **Kill rule**
is the result, written before a test runs, that shelves it.

## Verdict on the audit summary

The audit's direction is right, and v2 is a real improvement on v1. Its bottom line is too strong. The design **could**
test whether the support gap produces Q errors and whether BCA helps. It cannot do so **as written**, because v2 has no
outcome-independent decision rule for any outcome.

## Blocking before any next step

1. **No kill rule exists anywhere in v1, v2 or the audit.** The user's standing rule requires one before any new test,
   including the geometry-only coverage audit. v2 line 51 has a qualitative consequence ("report the failed design"),
   but no number decides it.
2. **Coverage pass marks are not frozen, and v2 lets them follow the audit.** v2 line 72 makes minimum thresholds
   "subject to the geometry-only audit". Line 86 guards the band criterion only against BCA outcomes, not against the
   coverage counts themselves. Freeze every coverage threshold before the audit decodes any array.
3. **The state-neighborhood support check cannot pass at radius 0.5.** A per-time Gaussian model of the training
   states reproduces the receipt's standard deviations within about 2% on all 13 coordinates. Under it, a probe has
   about 1 to 2 same-bin, same-side training rows within standardized distance 0.5, against the 32 rows from 8
   episodes required. Nearly every probe would be labeled "support unknown". A radius of about 0.67 to 0.77 gives the
   intended counts. The declared sensitivity pair at 0.4 and 0.6 does not reach it. Set the radius from a pre-declared
   outcome-free quantity before the audit, or measure state coverage in low-dimensional cells that match the mask
   (ten-step bin, load bins, current bins).
4. **No primary comparison, practical effect size or trained-seed count.** v2 lists contrasts across 4 cells, 3 data
   conditions and 5 checkpoints with equal standing. The tolerances `tau(v)` and `eta` are precision bands, not
   practical thresholds. One candidate primary is the paired change in absolute Q error, BCA minus native, on in-band
   high-load probes under recorded current at 20k in the hole condition, with the trained seed as the unit. With two
   seeds, the only honest reading is "both seeds positive beyond the band", which has probability 0.25 under no
   effect.

## Major design issues still open in v2

- **The band is nearly value-flat.** On the frozen plant, the finite-horizon linear-quadratic curvature in `a0` is
  0.672 return units per unit squared. Moving 0.05 from the optimum costs about 0.002 units, and the 0.1-wide band
  spans about 0.13 units near the optimum. Under simple neutral continuations, the value spread across the band and
  its two neighbors averages about 1 to 1.5 units, and its sign depends on the continuation. The full-data 20k critic
  already misjudges in-band commands at `g >= 0.4` by +19 to +82 units on the development banks. In-band versus
  adjacent decisions will therefore be near-ties, and the full-versus-hole accuracy change may be small against that
  baseline. Freeze a value-gap exposure threshold, in return units, before any labels exist. Read the native arm's
  value gate and record it before any BCA or control outcome is unsealed. If it fails, the method result is
  inconclusive.
- **The load threshold is a median split.** P(g >= 0.4) is exactly 0.5 at every step under the recorded law. About 29%
  of high-load rows have `g < 0.5`, and a 0.1 change in `g` moves a state only about 0.15 in standardized distance.
  Low-load rows with retained in-band labels therefore sit very close to many high-load probes. The same-side neighbor
  restriction at v2 line 86 hides this. Add predeclared load-depth strata, for example [0.4, 0.5), [0.5, 0.6) and
  ≥ 0.6. Either justify 0.4 as a deliberately shallow design or version a stricter threshold before any counts.
- **The knee is the strongest counter-thrust, not the static best command at this load.** Cancelling a load of 0.4
  needs thruster-1 thrust of only about −0.32 with all four thrusters or −0.40 alone. The knee command matches the
  load only near `g = 0.8` to `1.0`, which covers about 0.4% to 6% of recorded steps. An unconstrained
  linear-quadratic estimate puts the box-pinned optimum at `−k` for roughly a third to a half of high-load states. AUDIT
  finding 1 is worded narrowly enough to stand. Summaries calling the band "the counter-command" should not.
- **Trained actors may never enter the hole.** At all 128 development-bank states, the 20k actor's first command stayed
  between −0.48 and +0.52, the nearest 0.32 from the band center. Continued actors moved very little from it. v2
  correctly says no entry leaves the free-policy mechanism untested. It still needs a numeric minimum entry rate for
  the native arm, defined like the support pilot's exposure fraction, with the return claim shelved below it.
- **The within-model control cell is missing.** The factor table at v2 lines 100–103 has no "inside the band at low
  load" cell under recorded current, where labels are retained. Under the analytic expectation it holds about 1,920
  training rows, as many as the hole. Pairing high-load and low-load in-band probes inside one trained model is the
  cleanest way to separate a missing-label effect from the thrust kink at `a0 = −k`. That kink makes the function hard
  to fit at both load levels.
- **The replacement band check is weakly powered, and its pass mark is unfrozen.** The recorder ignores the state, so
  band counts in a fixed neighborhood are Binomial(n, 0.05). That gives 3.2 rows per band at 64 neighbors and 1.6 at
  32. The chance that at least one of the three bands is empty is 0.11 at 64 and 0.48 at 32. An empty band is
  therefore mostly chance, not a local support finding. Pool the alternative bands, or pool over neighborhoods, and
  freeze the threshold with its expected false-"unknown" rate.
- **Most of the execution path is new code.** These pieces already exist and are reusable: the fresh 10k native warmup
  and the fork into native, BCA at kappa 3 and matched distance arms (`support_shift_pilot_v1/runtime.py` lines
  186–202), condition-specific normalization, and the direct-dynamics reference continuation
  (`unifloral_frozen_usefulness_v1/simulation.py` lines 398–470). These are new: the conditional mask with an
  independent second implementation, a per-(episode, bin, load side) random-removal planner, a three-condition data
  plan, a branch schedule ending at 20k with refreshes at 15k and 20k only, in-loop snapshot export and a non-terminal
  snapshot loader, recorder-driven state banks, a band-structured candidate generator, strong-current whole-policy
  evaluation with per-step hole logging, and the error-accounting module with its identity fixtures. The existing
  bank builder rolls prefixes forward with the trained actor, which v2 forbids.
- **Time restoration must be stated as a mechanism.** The environment's reset always sets `t = 0` and has no time
  option. References at `t > 0` must replay a prefix to obtain the float64 state, then step the canonical dynamics
  directly with the remaining-fraction input set to `(100 − t − j)/100`, as the frozen evaluator does. Add a parity
  check at a `t > 0` probe. The existing check covers only `t = 0` and `t = 99`.
- **The budget has no ceiling.** v2's formulas check out: 120,000 × S critic updates, 21 × S refreshes and 33 × S unique
  actor snapshots. An **estimate** from the two Zeus 10k warmup receipts (637.6 s and 708.3 s) puts training alone at
  about 4.3 to 5.0 hours for two seeds. That is 1.7 to 2 times the support pilot's 9,000-second cap, before reference
  rollouts, state banks, evaluation and compilation. The Zeus hold in `titan_handoff_20261027/ATTEMPT_HOLD.json`
  (execution not allowed) must also be resolved by the user.

## Corrections to the v2 audit and draft

- **The fork already exists.** v2 line 128 says the continuation wiring "is not implemented by the existing kappa-zero
  scheduler". That is true of `training.py`, but `support_shift_pilot_v1/runtime.py` already performs the 10k fork. The
  work is to extend it. `training.py` writes no checkpoints itself, but its supervisor saves every 1,000 updates for its
  single fixed configuration.
- **AUDIT finding 3 mislabels which differences are systematic.** The recorder's command ignores the state, and load
  changes without any action input. So within each (episode, bin, load side) stratum, removing the band is independent
  of the current observation. Remaining state and normalization differences between the hole and random pools are
  chance. Reward and successor differences are systematic, because reward and next state depend on the executed command.
  Trajectory history is identical across all three pools, since they are row subsets of the same 768 episodes. The
  random pool also keeps about 95% of in-band high-load labels, which the hole pool removes entirely.
- **Calibration reuse is adaptive, not only non-fresh.** With kappa above zero, the 10k threshold shapes training until
  15k. The 15k and 20k refreshes then reuse rows that already influenced the model.
- **The calibration "design failure" gate can never trigger.** An episode has no row outside the hole with probability
  at most 0.05^100. The real gap is a replacement rule. About 3.2 of the existing seed-2026102362 calibration rows are
  expected to fall inside the hole. Freeze an outcome-independent replacement, for example the next eligible step
  cyclically, and state whether all three conditions share one calibration row set.
- **The calibration identity is already exact on disk.** It is the first 128 tuning-validation groups sorted by group ID
  (`unifloral_checkpoint_v1/data.py` line 43), with IDs in `bca_one_sided_v1/run/data_receipt.json`. The other 128
  tuning-validation episodes were used as development validation in the one-sided work. They should be labeled
  development-consumed, not "unassigned".
- **Executed commands never land exactly on the band edges.** In float32, the executed hole runs from −0.84999996 to
  −0.75000006. The values −0.75 and −0.85000002 both fall outside. Exact-boundary fixtures must be synthetic float64
  requests. Also add a fixture where the float32 load and the float64 load fall on opposite sides of 0.4.
- **The stall-boundary assay was never run.** The manifest lists it as prepared but unrun, so importing it yields a
  protocol, not evidence. The other four Titan items are reported results that are absent here.
- **The 4D ball was a reported count in v1, not a gate.** AUDIT finding 2 calls the v1 ball "that gate", but v1's only
  stated pass mark was the 32-row, 8-episode state neighborhood.
- **A Bayesian-specific claim needs more than a strength match.** The distance control never uses the calibrated
  reference in its penalty. It shares only shadow auxiliary work and a one-time gradient match at 10k. Isolating the
  Bayesian part would need a non-Bayesian quantile arm with the same refresh behavior.
- **Plain language.** OOD, ID, MAE, RMSE, RMS, BC, twin-min, U, BNP and AUROC are undefined at first use. Add a short
  glossary.

## What v2 gets right

One width instead of a sweep. The four-action ball is removed as a gate. Independent mask fixtures and contamination
counts are required. Mechanics, coverage and value exposure are kept as separate evidence levels, and support novelty is
labeled apart from overestimation. Causal claims are narrowed. The warmup and fork are explicit, and the estimand is
renamed offline learning progress. Tolerance and tie rules and their quantiles are frozen. Refusals are counted on a
shared valid subset. Per-step hole-entry records are kept. The 768 training episodes are labeled as development data.
The budget formulas are correct.

## Suggested next steps, none involving training

Each step needs its kill rule written and approved at the user's terminal before it runs. The values below are
placeholders for the user to set, not proposals of specific numbers.

1. **Seed and role ledger from local JSON only.** Any overlap of a new range with a known range, or any unresolved role
   label, blocks freezing test seeds. Label the ledger "Zeus-visible only; Titan collisions unverified".
2. **Synthetic fixtures.** Two independent mask implementations on hand-built float32 and float64 cases, plus the
   accounting identities. Any disagreement stops the work.
3. **Freeze the decision block.** This covers the primary comparison, the practical effect sizes for error, regret and
   return, the value-gap and free-entry thresholds, the coverage thresholds and support radius, the seed count S and the
   runtime ceiling.
4. **Geometry-only coverage audit.** It decodes training and tuning arrays only and needs the user's approval. Zero
   forbidden labels is required. Below the frozen hole-row, episode or neighborhood-pass minimums, the region is shelved
   and a new version is proposed, with no search over regions.
5. **Calibration and random-removal row plans with a balance report.** An imbalance above the frozen bound is reported
   as an imbalanced control. It is never redrawn.
6. **Budget sheet for approval.** A projected total above the ceiling reduces S or the counts before an approval request.
