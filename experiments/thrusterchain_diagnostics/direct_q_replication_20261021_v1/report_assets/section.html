Define the premise before the mechanism

BCA is the existing Bayesian calibration procedure. Its native intervention changes behavior-cloning strength. Here we study the proposed direct score Q1 minus uncertainty width, without updating either network. Earlier tests established the proposed live-width derivative mechanically; a derivative alone does not establish useful uncertainty.

The primary error is e = Q1 − true Q of the original actor continuation. Larger relative error should receive a larger penalty if the penalty is to correct ordering. This is signed-error Pearson alignment; it is a different endpoint from the earlier positive-error Spearman analysis. Pearson over all 12 indexed candidates equals the normalized dot product of every i<j difference, without recentering the differences. That equality is a code invariant, not a second independent piece of evidence.

What the intervals can describe
