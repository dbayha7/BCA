# What η learns, and two proposed signal variants

**η is fitted to aggregate soft coverage of a changing one-step TD target. It is not pointwise regression to absolute TD error, an established conditional quantile, or uncertainty about true policy Q.** The smallest proposed next test is option A below. Option B is closer to long-return value error but requires an additional fitted recursion and stronger offline support assumptions. Neither is implemented or authorized here as a new training run.

## Exact implemented signal

For a logged transition `(x,a,r,x',m)`, `x` contains13 physical/environment coordinates, observed regime and remaining-time fraction; `m=0` at the inherent H=100 terminal boundary and1 otherwise. The training adapter supplies `done=1−m`, raw rewards and actions in[-1,1]. It normalizes the first13 observation coordinates with training-only mean and `std+.001`, leaving regime/time unchanged at this layer. The calibrator then divides every already-normalized observation coordinate by1.001 because its own configured mean/std are0/1; it concatenates the4 action coordinates. Two64-unit ReLU layers and a scalar head produce `η=softplus(logη)+.01`. Inputs exclude reward, successor, current Q and residual. [Adapter, lines219–282](/C:/Users/David/Documents/Codex/2026-10-20/task/results_20261021/release/ThrusterChain_results_20261021/source/experiments/thrusterchain_diagnostics/training.py:219); [initialization, lines129–141](/C:/Users/David/Documents/Codex/2026-10-20/task/results_20261021/release/ThrusterChain_results_20261021/source/algorithms/td3_bc_bca.py:129); [network, lines8–37](/C:/Users/David/Documents/Codex/2026-10-20/task/results_20261021/release/ThrusterChain_results_20261021/source/calibration/network.py:8).

At each accepted training step, the scale fit uses the pre-update native networks:

```text
ε ~ Normal(0,.2² I), clipped coordinatewise to[-.5,.5]
a' = clip(π_target(x') + ε, -1,1)
y = stopgrad[r + m * min(Q1_target,Q2_target)(x',a')]   (γ=1)
q = stopgrad[min(Q1,Q2)(x,a)]
d = (y-q)/u
w_i = Exp(1)_i / sum_j Exp(1)_j
c_i = sigmoid(20*(d_i+η_i)) * sigmoid(20*(η_i-d_i))
Lη = (sum_i w_i*c_i - .9)² + .005*sum_i w_i*η_i²
u_new = .99*u + .01*max(std_batch(y-q),1e-6), initially u=1
```

Only calibrator parameters receive this loss's gradients; targets, current q and bootstrap weights are detached. Adam learning rate is.001. The unit is the moving **standard deviation**, not mean absolute error or RMS. The scale fit uses a separate folded noise key from the native critic update. This loss trades batch-average coverage against width; it does not enforce conditional90% coverage at every `(x,a)`. Here residual variation mixes fitting error, changing online/target models, deliberately injected target-action noise, and state/action variation—even though the plant itself is deterministic. Bootstrap weighting and R do not separate these components into epistemic Q uncertainty. A one-step residual can cancel or accumulate along a policy trajectory; its absolute size alone does not determine signed long-return or advantage error.

## Exactly two proposed variants

The normalized exponential training weights above are Dirichlet/Bayesian-bootstrap weights, **not target/source importance ratios**. Before either option could launch, verify an untouched existing offline episode pool exists; otherwise separately authorize logger data and declare whole-episode fit/calibration/evaluation splits. Neither availability nor collection is implied here. Unused transitions from the already adaptive128 reserved episodes are not an independent episode holdout. Current diagnostic/evaluator banks remain evaluation evidence and cannot be repurposed as training labels.

| Proposed variant | New signal and smallest loss change | Exact offline-only requirements and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Frozen-policy local overestimation signal — recommended smallest next test** | Freeze100k Q1 and the unsmoothed canonical actor. Compute `δ=Q1(x,a)−[r+m*Q1(x',π(x'))]` on logged transitions. Fit a nonnegative head h(x,a) with **pointwise pinball loss** at a predeclaredτ=.9 to `z=max(δ,0)`: `L=mean ρ_.9(z−h)`. This is a new loss and target, not a refit of the aggregate BCA objective. Do not reuse old R as its calibration. | Need logged `(episode_id,t,x,a,r,x',m)`, frozen normalizer/Q1/actor hashes, and whole-episode signal-fit/calibration/evaluation partitions fixed before constructing labels. No simulator or true-Q labels, target smoothing, or actor training. A separate calibration pool is needed only if a separately named residual interval is requested. The existing128 bank was already reused adaptively; it cannot become fresh by relabeling. Previously unconsumed archived episodes are needed for independence from the original model's training/history; otherwise label results conditional/in-sample for that frozen model. h is only a local one-step positive-residual predictor. It need not align with signed Qπ error or improve action choice. |
| **B. Signed residual propagation / fitted value-error corrector** | Freeze the same Q1/π and δ above. Fit a **signed** head g with finite-horizon Bellman regression `L=mean[g_t(x,a)−stopgrad(δ_t+m*g_{t+1}(x',π(x')))]²`, terminal continuation0. The exact policy-operator identity is `Q1−Qπ = δ + Pπ(Q1−Qπ)` when δ denotes its conditional expected residual. Thus this seeks a propagated value-error corrector, equivalently a form of fitted policy evaluation. If a nonnegative penalty is later desired, take `max(g,0)` **after** signed propagation; summing positive local residuals changes the target by discarding cancellation. | Need the same complete offline tuples, correct time/boundary masks, episode-disjoint fit/evaluation pools and successor coverage for π; no logged next-action substitution is required because π supplies the queried action. Backward time fitting or a fixed fitted-evaluation schedule is an additional algorithm, to be declared before any run. It relies on function approximation, coverage and projected-Bellman assumptions, and may inherit FQE bias. It is not automatically calibrated uncertainty. Logged behavior returns estimate behavior continuation, not Qπ, and cannot be substituted as labels. |

**Shared hard limitation:** this logger has625 categorical actions, while the frozen deterministic actor is generally off-grid. Off-grid successor actions and candidate actions have zero categorical logging probability. Merely querying a network there supplies no outcome evidence; arbitrary off-grid Qπ is not identified from these logs without additional structural/generalization assumptions. Projecting or randomizing the target policy onto supported actions would change the estimand and requires an explicit new choice. Neither option uses evaluator-only oracle labels to bypass that limitation. [Logger probabilities, lines60–80](/C:/Users/David/Documents/Codex/2026-10-20/task/results_20261021/release/ThrusterChain_results_20261021/source/experiments/thrusterchain_diagnostics/training_data.py:60).

**Decision:** choose A only if the next question is whether a directly fitted, policy/head-aligned local signal is more useful than η. Choose B only if the question is propagated policy-value error and the offline extrapolation assumptions are acceptable. Keep the actor/critic fixed and the variant explicitly named. Changing R or its nominal level alone is dose tuning, not either signal redesign. No implementation, extra sweep, new training, or Q guarantee is implied by this note.
