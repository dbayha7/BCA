## Findings and disposition

No material numerical or scientific defect remains in the reviewed aggregate. The text now distinguishes each method's own policy-continuation Q target from a shared critic-estimation target, and distinguishes empirical coverage on a designed diagnostic query law from coverage under the behavior calibration law. Those qualifications are necessary for interpreting the results.

The alignment report and final HTML now state that penalty alignment measures the native **behavior-cloning multiplier**, not the complete actor penalty or its gradient. A larger multiplier need not produce a larger BC loss because the distance to the logged action also varies. Native BCA computes its training multiplier at the logged action (`algorithms/td3_bc_bca.py:283-310`); the six-candidate frozen-readout diagnostic does not reconstruct training weights or show direct penalties imposed on those commands. The 154 counterexamples disprove a universal ordering of that multiplier by candidate opportunity loss; they do not establish harmful regularization or a calibration-theorem failure.

## Interpretation supported by the evidence

At 100,000 updates, pooled BCA-minus-TD3+BC mean returns are full **-3.857** (95% paired-t interval **[-14.302, 6.588]**), rare **-3.697** (**[-8.447, 1.052]**), and zero **-5.998** (**[-8.597, -3.399]**). These use five trained replicates, not 400 independent training replicates. The intervals are exploratory, conditional on fixed evaluation banks, and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Four of five full and zero pairs confound host with arm; all rare pairs ran on Zeus.

Pooled native query-band coverage across the 15 BCA runs ranges from **54.5% to 75.3%**, below nominal 90% on this explicitly designed query law. This is empirical undercoverage on that law, not by itself a contradiction of the calibration theory under its assumptions. Q-error comparisons involve each learned policy's own continuation and own sixth candidate. Candidate ranking loss is over six actions, not the entire continuous action space. Numerical QP bounds concern the fixed evaluation starts, not a continuous population optimum. Known-model LQR controls are privileged, untrained controls rather than data-matched learned baselines. These results concern one frozen native BCA configuration and do not establish failure of BCA in general.
