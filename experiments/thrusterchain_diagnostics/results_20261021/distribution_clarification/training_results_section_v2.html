What was trained, and what is being compared

The core compares the repository's native TD3+BC host with its native TD3+BC+BCA variant on continuous ThrusterChain. There are five independent data/optimization replicates, three retention conditions and two arms: 30 planned jobs. This is the task-specific BCA objective at the predeclared 100,000-update budget, not every possible BCA formulation. Both actuator regimes and both load levels are present in each training distribution. These are real neural learner runs, distinct from the finite companion's enumerated dynamic-programming smoke results. Other host algorithms, context-frequency shifts and placement ablations are outside this core.

The objective is raw undiscounted reward over 100 steps, with zero terminal continuation. An observation has the 13 physical coordinates, the observed episode-fixed regime and remaining-time fraction. Network arithmetic and deployed observations/actions are float32; the common plant executes in float64. The horizon mask stops the Bellman target even though the physical environment reports a time-limit truncation.

Paired results across five trained replicates

Each cell starts from a policy's mean over the same frozen start bank, then compares matched methods within a trained replicate. The table reports the mean and sample standard deviation of BCA-minus-TD3+BC differences across all five trained replicates. A positive difference favors BCA for that condition. The exploratory 95% paired-t intervals use 4 degrees of freedom, condition on the shared evaluation banks, and are unadjusted for the many retention/context comparisons. With five replicates they should be read cautiously, especially where host and arm are confounded. An incomplete pair remains visible, and five-replicate aggregates are withheld unless all five pairs are present.

The replication unit is the trained replicate, not an evaluation rollout. The final bank has 100 starts per regime/load context, 400 in total, shared across methods and replicates. These bank means are sample estimates of the declared continuous-start distribution, not exact population expectations. Pooling gives each of the four contexts equal weight and can conceal gains and losses in different regimes.

Return, regret, failure and action use

All rows are available without JavaScript.

$policy

Failure is an absolute position coordinate above 3 at any pre-step or post-step state, or an incomplete/nonfinite rollout. It is measured without early termination; finite rollouts continue to step 100. Above-knee frequency and critical-region frequency describe executed actions and visits, not categorical behavior support.

$oracle

Regret is reported as numerical lower/upper bounds relative to a constrained continuous QP at each shared start. Its Frank–Wolfe gap and replay error are numerical checks, not interval arithmetic or symbolic exactness. Averages of these fixed-start bounds do not certify the continuous population optimum.

Fixed controls on the same banks

$controls

These are untrained model-based controls: they use the known dynamics' finite-horizon LQR gains and apply the stated action cap. They are not data-matched learned baselines, and their known-model information prevents a claim of equal information or learning fairness. The fixed-knee cap tests indiscriminate exclusion of high commands. The regime-aware cap permits useful monotone-regime commands while respecting the stall knee. These controls need no fitting and are evaluated even when a learned job fails. They are neither trained-method replicates nor the constrained optimal policy.

Retained data and the reserved calibration bank

Each replicate generates 1,024 source episodes of 100 steps, exactly 256 in each regime/load context, with randomized order. Physical starts and action draws are independent across episodes conditional on the balanced context assignment; the sequence of contexts is not an i.i.d. mixture draw. A seeded permutation reserves the same 128 whole episode IDs before retention, leaving 896 episode IDs for optimization in both methods.

The behavior chooses commands from five atoms per coordinate. Its center is the nearest atom to the knee-clipped 0.6-scaled finite-horizon LQR command; each coordinate samples the center with probability 0.8 and a uniform atom with probability 0.2. Recorded probabilities are categorical masses. A continuous neural actor generally proposes actions outside those atoms: full retention does not create continuous action support, and ordinary action-density ratios cannot repair that mismatch.

The pre-outcome critical rule is current at least 0.8 and any absolute command above the knee. Full, rare and zero retention keep critical rows with probabilities 1, 0.05 and 0, using the same source log and retention random stream. Rows are never bridged or replenished. Both methods use the same retained training rows and training-only normalization within a condition; normalization is fitted separately by retention. Host initialization and random streams are paired, with a separate BCA stream.

$data $critical_note

One retained row is selected without using outcomes from each reserved episode. An empty reserved episode contributes no row, without replacement or replenishment. The same bank is reused for 18 adaptive refreshes. A pooled numerical threshold-size check does not establish context-conditional coverage, and the reused bank is not a fresh independent holdout at each refresh. The shared episode reservation also does not remove the dependence imposed by balanced context assignment.

Policy-value error and action ranking

Distribution label: joint state/time-and-action stress test. These queries reuse start-law physical states at later time labels and change the command law. They are neither behavior-calibration draws nor a controlled action-only shift. Compare the source and target laws.

The independent critic-query bank contains 48 query states: four physical starts per context crossed with times 0, 50 and 99. Six candidate commands are checked at each query: zero, all-negative knee, all-positive knee, all-negative one, all-positive one and the deployed actor command. A reference Q forces that first command, then deterministically rolls out the deployed policy to step 100. It is policy Q, not optimal Q. Each method therefore has its own policy-continuation target and its own sixth candidate action: cross-method Q-error differences do not isolate estimation quality against a common target.

$critic

Ranking regret compares the true return of the critic's preferred candidate with the best of these six candidates. It is not regret over all possible continuous actions. Pairwise accuracy excludes true ties. This designed query law is not the actor's occupancy distribution. The separate one-step Bellman residual uses the deployed actor and learned twin-min continuation; it need not equal the noisy native target residual evaluated in the next section.

Native residual bands, usability and refusal

Read the coverage table as a joint-query, frozen-reference diagnostic—not an IID calibration test.

“Full” means no critical-row deletion. Its 128-row behavior calibration bank still differs from the query bank in state/time sampling and action sampling. The threshold, scale and residual unit were frozen at 95k, while the tested native residual uses the 100k critic and target networks. These changes are not isolated by the current coverage measurement. The proposed action-only check holds those other factors fixed.

BCA uses one pooled, uniformly weighted bank across both regimes and both loads. Each score is abs(noisy native one-step TD target − current twin-min Q) / positive learned scale. A single global weighted Bayesian conformal prediction threshold is fitted to those scores. The scale takes state and action as inputs; that does not turn the global threshold into a groupwise conditional certificate. This is neither critical-only calibration nor a Mondrian procedure.

The native band's target is the current noisy TD3 one-step target minus current twin-min Q. Its nominal residual coverage is 90%, with posterior credibility 95%; these numbers have different meanings. The report checks the frozen checkpoint reference on an independent designed query bank. This is empirical residual coverage, not long-horizon Q coverage or a new generalization guarantee. This designed distribution of states, times and candidate actions differs from the behavior calibration bank. Coverage below 90% on this diagnostic distribution is not, by itself, a contradiction of the calibration theory's assumptions.

$calibration

The final reference generally comes from the last refresh at update 95,000. An unready reference or a ready infinite threshold is not a usable calibrated intervention. Native infinite-threshold fallback applies a unit behavior-cloning multiplier; it does not refuse the actor's command or apply maximal caution. “Band refusal” below counts lack of both a ready reference and native dose usability. Dose usability is not certified statistical support or population action support. Infinite widths can make coverage vacuous.

An initially insufficient bank would refuse the entire BCA training arm, leaving no trained policy for it. All prepared banks in this frozen run currently pass the pooled numerical size check; this says nothing about critical-group calibration. The scientific comparison is the utility of a global calibration intervention under critical thinning, with local diagnostics. It does not validate critical-region uncertainty.

Configuration, execution and source records

Twenty-two jobs were trained and evaluated on Zeus. Eight Titan CPU jobs are represented by the imported, hash-verified report; their rollouts were not independently rerun in this Zeus-only results session. Both hosts used CPU, but host, operating system, Python patch and ancillary environment differences remain. Four of five full-retention pairs put BCA on Titan, and four of five zero-retention pairs put TD3+BC on Titan. This confounds host with method in those conditions. Passing a fixed numerical fixture does not remove that confounding or guarantee identical training trajectories. All rare-retention pairs ran on Zeus.
