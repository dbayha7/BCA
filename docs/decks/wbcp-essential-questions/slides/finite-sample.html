A finite-sample effect of the method: explained, not fixed.

Heavy weights (33–39× the mean) sit on the misses. Banks that miss them report a posterior spread about a third too small.

The excess shrinks as the bank grows but stays above 5%.

This sweep is hopper only, with independent rows; the other hosts come in question 4.

Estimated weights add their own cost: walker2d fails 14.9% at n = 8,192 with no shift; 10× more fitting data gives 5.7%.
