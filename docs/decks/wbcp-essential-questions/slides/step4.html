Question 7 · Where should the signal go?

What does signal-study step 4 test?

P1

BC amplification

Scale each row's BC weight by the signal.

P2

Actor-Q trust

Down-weight the actor's Q term where the band is wide.

P3

Critic-side bound

The actor climbs a lower bound, Q1 − c·Ŵ.

Held fixed

Q1's calibrated band as the signal. Strength is matched on realized policy displacement, not mean dose.

Controls

The same weights shuffled (also within strata of per-row gradient size), reversed, or an unrelated signal; oracles; plain BC at every strength.

Data crossed

Expert, medium, mixed and poor actions on identical states, plus three other blocks.

Pilot (audited addendum, Oct 2). All checks pass. The expert-data positive control has no power (oracle − shuffled = −0.004 / −0.033); with poor data it does (0.166 / 0.082). The G2 gate needs a decision before the main run.

Forecast. Sceptical: no placement is expected to show real targeting on the natural misleading cases.
