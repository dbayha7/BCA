What theory predicts (written for this deck, not pre-registered)

No shift: BQ‑CP, RCPS and exact-weight WBCP fail ≤ 5%; W‑CRC far more, by design; estimated weights only if the classifier's noise leaves a non-pivotal score alone.

A shift that moves the score's law: shift-blind rules fail; WBCP stays near 5%, a little above at strong shift, or abstains.

Dependent banks: every rule can fail, since all of them assume independent rows.
