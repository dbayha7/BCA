How does BCA run, from training to testing?

Training

The host runs unchanged, except for one detached multiplier on one loss term.

Calibration

At each of 198 refreshes, BCA scores the held-out bank with the current critic and sets R by WBCP.

Testing

Validity and the known-MDP test are done. Placement is running. Full training needs approval.
