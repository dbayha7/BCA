Part 1 · The models

Three kinds of guarantee

Marginal

W‑CRC

Miscoverage ≤ α on average over banks. A single bank can be far over α, so our test fails it often, by design.

High-probability

RCPS · BQ‑CP

At least 95% of banks have miscoverage ≤ α when rows are exchangeable. RCPS proves it with a concentration bound; for BQ‑CP, 95% credibility equals 95% confidence, so its 5% is exact.

Posterior credibility

WBCP with weights

Given this bank, P(miscoverage ≤ α) ≥ 95% (Corollary 3). The frequentist bound (Theorem 4) adds slack of 0.32 even with no shift at n = 1,103, so counting banks is an empirical check.

Our test, at most 5% of banks failing, is the high-probability standard.
