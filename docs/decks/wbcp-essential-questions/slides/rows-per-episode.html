A few rows per episode, and fewer where ρ is high.

Hopper holds near 5% up to K = 10. Walker2d already fails 7.5% at K = 5.

The design effect D = 1 + (K − 1)ρ predicts thinned-bank failure within ±2 points on all five datasets.

The cost is withheld training data (about 10.7% of hopper at K = 5), not band width. Its effect on training is unmeasured.
