Recording controller
The recording controller is the fixed controller that made the training data. It drives normally, within 80%, but in the full-throttle region it flips a coin in each state and on half of them puts thruster 1 at full throttle. It is the 0 of the points scale.
Used in: pipeline step 1, the points scale, the recorded data.
