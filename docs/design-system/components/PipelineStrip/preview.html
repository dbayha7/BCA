1. 1Collect the data

    The recording controller, a fixed controller, drives 100-step episodes in the recorded conditions of the stalling world. Each step becomes one row of the data.

    The recorded data

  2. 2Remove commands

    Main test: the densest-15% removal deletes the recording controller's full-throttle cluster.

    The distribution shift

  3. 3Train TD3+BC

    On the rows that are left, TD3+BC trains a critic, which scores every command in every state, and with it an actor.

    Methods

  4. 4Add BCA's band

    BCA builds a band, a range around the critic's estimate, sized on recorded rows kept out of training.

    The BCA test

  5. 5Test the actor

    Every actor is scored on each situation's 1,000 fixed starts: recorded, strong current, big waves, big starting pushes.
