Past 80% the push falls. In the stalling world a thruster pushes 0.65 at 90% throttle and 0.5 at full throttle, while its throttle cost keeps rising. The hardest push a thruster can give is 0.8, at 80%.

The recording controller

The fixed controller that made the training data. It drives normally, within 80%, but in the full-throttle region it flips a coin in each state and on half of them puts thruster 1 at full throttle. It is the 0 of the points scale.

The stop rule

The test stopped if perfectly placed caution did not beat three controls by at least 3 points in some shifted situation.
