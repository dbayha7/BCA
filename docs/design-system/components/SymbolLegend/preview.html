What the symbols mean

dj
float j's distance from rest, the length of the line from the center of its map to its point
pj, vj
float j's position number and speed number; both are 0 when the float is at rest
j
a float, numbered 1 to 5

Also new in this step

start 0
the first of the 1,000 fixed starting states of the recorded-conditions evaluation bank (index 0)
recorded
the recorded conditions, in distribution

Worked example. At start 0, float 1 has p1 = −0.3216 and v1 = 0.0686.
