Evidence, sources and gaps

The results manifest lists every experiment with its status, the numbers this interface displays and the files they come from. The build resolves each number from its source at assembly time and refuses to publish if one cannot be found. Paths are relative to the repository root of branch codex/thrusterchain-bca-handoff-20261026.

Remaining evidence gaps

  1. Titan-only folders. The held-out evaluation, the explicit OOD diagnostic, the adaptive-signal screen and recovery, and the reported support-pilot completion are not in this checkout. Their rows show the user's report and no numbers.
  2. Independent seeds. No completed Unifloral-era result on this machine compares independently initialized policies.
  3. State shift. The three shifted start families are defined and unused; every reference and return here is under the recorded law.
  4. Separately trained controls on the banks. The decision banks score one checkpoint with alternative scores; separately trained native and distance models exist only in the policy pilot.
  5. The checkpoint's own calibration numbers. Threshold, empirical quantile and posterior quantile at update 20,000 are inside the runtime assets and were not extracted.
  6. Coverage after training. Containment of the frozen band on the held-out bank is reported for the development transitions, not for the calibration bank itself after the last refresh.
  7. Stall-boundary validity assay. Prepared; no run receipt.
