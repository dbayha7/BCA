# M9. The lower edge

**Verdict: does not work** (the arithmetic is right and tested; with BCA's own band the penalty is about 1% of the
critic's error at the actor's command, and the trained actor does not move; the classifier-weighted version moves the
actor but by penalizing every command outside the recorded range, and it loses to a hand-set range limit by 46 points).

**The one sentence for the advisor.** *The lower edge subtracts, from the critic's value at the actor's command, how
much wider BCA's band is there than at the recorded command; every line of that arithmetic reproduces the records bit

caution scores −13.2 and BCA's switched ramp 87.4; the same penalty fed with the critic's exact error wins back 94 of the 99
lost points (Stage 2's perfect band), so the lever works and the band is what fails to drive it.*

## 1. What the module does, in plain words

BCA puts an error band around a critic. The **critic** Q1 is the learned estimate of how much return a command earns
from a state (TD3+BC trains two; the lower edge reads the first). The **band** is Q1 ± U, where U is the **half-width**
(the "width" below): U(s, a) = R · u_f · max(η_f(s, a), 10⁻⁶), with R the threshold (module M5), u_f the unit and
η_f the error-size network (module M2), both frozen at the last refresh. The **actor** π is the controller being
trained; π(s) is its command at state s; a_i is the **recorded command** of training row i.

The lower edge is one way of *using* the band. In every actor update it adds a penalty to the actor's objective:

    W_i = [ U(s_i, π(s_i)) − U(s_i, a_i) ]_+          ([x]_+ means max(x, 0))

the width at the actor's own command minus the width at the recorded command of the same state, kept only where
positive. The actor then climbs the **lower edge** Q1 − k·W instead of Q1:

    loss = −λ · mean_i [ Q1(s_i, π(s_i)) − k · W_i ] + mean_i mean_j (π(s_i)_j − a_ij)²,      λ = α / mean_i |Q1|

The second term is TD3+BC's own **cloning term** (the pull toward the recorded command). k is the penalty scale, 1 in
every run (2 a secondary arm). The gradient reaches the actor's parameters only through η_f's command input; η_f, u_f,
R and U(s_i, a_i) are constants. "Paired" means measured against the recorded command, so the penalty is zero when the
actor repeats the recording and never rewards a command.

**Where it was used.**
- *The weighting test* (`experiments/shift_iw`, specification version 5): built and unit-tested, the **oracle lower
  edge** run (the same penalty with the exact one-step error of the critic in place of the band: marks L1, L2), BCA's
  own lower-edge arms **never trained** (L3 "not run" on all five qualities, because L2's predicted return was below
  the bar). A **proxy check** compared BCA's penalty with the oracle's before any training.
- *Stage 2* (`outputs/shift/beyond-the-data/stage2`, pre-registration version 6, seeds 0-4, exploratory by its own
  rule): the standard band failed the pre-registered detection marks (direction 0.50 / 0.73, size 0.0004), so the
  pre-registered fallback, the **classifier-weighted band**, was declared, and the arm that trained ("bca_k1", k = 1)
  used that band (`policy/seed<K>/lock.json`: version "weighted", arm kind "bca_w"). Its width is
  U~(s, a) = R_c(w) · u_f · max(η_f, 10⁻⁶) + U_max · softplus(log w − l*), with w(s, a) the weight a frozen
  classifier of "recorded or not" gives the command.
- *The near-80 run* (version 8, seeds 0-4, the exploratory look): the arm "version6_bca", the paired lower edge at
  k = 1 on the **standard band** (arm kind "bca_u"), trained beside BCA's switched ramp (module M11) and the rivals.

**Refusal** (the band cannot certify, R = +infinity): the weighting test gives the row the largest finite threshold of
its table; Stage 2 and near-80 set the width to U_max, the largest width over the held-out rows. No refusal occurred in
any of these runs (every recorded band certified; `refused_rows` 0 in the weighting test).

**Strength** is how far a trained actor moved from the no-caution actor: the root-mean-square change of the command
over 5,000 held-out states. **Points**: 0 is the recording controller, 100 the world's best scripted controller.
**The stalling world**: a thruster's push falls past 80% throttle, so full throttle is harmful; **the no-stall world**:
it is not. **Strong current** is the shifted test situation.

## 2. What "works as intended" means (checkable statements)

- **C1, the arithmetic.** (a) The width read at the actor's command is BCA's own U = R · u_f · max(η_f, 10⁻⁶),
  equal to the recorded width readout. (b) The paired penalty is never negative and is exactly 0 at the recorded
  command. (c) With k = 0 the step is the plain TD3+BC actor step. (d) The penalty's gradient reaches the command only
  through η_f's command input. (e) A per-row threshold scales the penalty row by row, W(R_i) = (R_i / R_u) · W(R_u).
  (f) Refusal maps to the declared fallback.
- **C2, the purpose.** The penalty at the actor's command should measure the critic's over-optimism there: against the
  exact one-step error of the critic (the "oracle" penalty) it should have a ratio of means near 1 at k = 1 and a
  positive correlation (the proxy check, `spec.json` `lower_edge.proxy_check`); on hard-water states it should rise
  past 80% throttle and move the best throttle of Q1 − W below 80% (check (e)).
- **C3, the effect.** With k = 1 the trained actor moves (strength of the order of the matched rivals) and recovers
  points in strong current in the stalling world, without losing them in recorded conditions or in the no-stall world
  (Stage 2's marks M1-M5; the near-80 look's points).

**S4. The mechanism, with a known truth.** One throttle a, recorded command 0.75, the knee at 0.80. The critic keeps
rising past the knee with slope s_Q = 2 critic-value units per unit throttle (the order of the recorded preference,
section 5), while the truth falls there, so the right answer is a throttle of at most 0.80. The band is flat below the
knee and rises past it with slope s_U. The actor loss of line M9.4 with λ = 0.32 (the recorded value, α = 5 over
|Q1| ≈ 15.5) is minimized exactly and by gradient descent:

| band slope / critic slope | 0 | 0.01 | 0.03 | 0.1 | 0.3 | 0.5 | 0.7 | 0.85 | 1 | 1.5 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| throttle the actor settles at, k = 1 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.974 | 0.910 | 0.846 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 |
| the same, k = 2 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.878 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 | 0.800 |

Closed form: past the knee the actor settles at a_i + λ(s_Q − k·s_U)/2, capped at full throttle; it stays at the knee
only when k·s_U ≥ s_Q − 2(0.80 − 0.75)/λ, that is when the band's slope is at least 0.84 times the critic's at k = 1
(0.42 at k = 2). The lever works exactly as the arithmetic says: its effect is the band's slope in the command, and
nothing else. Section 5 measures that slope on the records: 0.01 of the critic's for the standard band.

## 7. Consistent and exceptional cases

**Consistent (every record).**
1. The standard band is flat across commands: effective k at most 0.005 on 10 of 10 seed-sets in Stage 2, at most
   0.04 on 25 of 25 seed-qualities in the weighting test; the penalty term stays at 0.5-1.0% of the cloning term in all
   10 near-80 seed-worlds.
2. The actor with the standard band does not move: strength 0.020-0.023 on 10 of 10 seed-worlds; points within 1 of no
   caution's in strong current and within 0.1 in recorded conditions.
3. The same penalty with an exact error works where the critic's over-optimism is the problem: the perfect band
   wins back 94 of the 99 lost points in Stage 2 (79.7 against −14.3 for no caution), the oracle lower edge +8.5 and
   +5.8 points on expert and medium-expert in the weighting test, and the perfect band costs 1.3 points in the no-stall
   world.
4. k = 0 reproduces the no-caution actor bit for bit (20 of 20 seed-worlds; the unit tests).
5. The classifier-weighted band penalizes every command outside the recorded range, harmful or not (share penalized
   1.00 on every seed; width 0.02-0.14 above the recorded command's at harmless commands), and in strong current its
   rank correlation with harm is negative on 5 of 5 seeds.

**Exceptional (a few records).**
6. Check (e) on BCA's standard penalty failed on 5 of 25 seed-qualities (e4 on expert seeds 1 and 3, medium-expert
   seed 0, random seed 2; e2-e3 on medium seed 4): the rise past 80% is real but so small that whether Q1 − W changes
   any best throttle is a matter of rounding.
7. On three of five seeds the standard band is narrower at the harmful full-throttle command than at its cap
   (median slope ratio −0.005, −0.035, −0.017): there the penalty's size form is zero on most harmful pairs.
8. The oracle lower edge failed L1 on medium, medium-replay and random (−0.8 to −1.1 points): on those
   qualities the recorded data already hold past-80% commands, so the one-step error is not where the loss comes from;
   this is a limit of the lever itself, not of the band.
9. No refusal ever occurred (all bands certified), so line M9.5 has never acted on real data; its consequence under the
   U_max rule (a refused standard band gives no caution) is shown only synthetically (S3).

## 9. What to say to the advisor

1. The lower edge is a penalty in the actor's objective equal to how much wider BCA's band is at the actor's own
   command than at the recorded one. Every piece of that arithmetic is tested and reproduces the records bit for bit
   (the width, the positive part, k = 0 equals plain TD3+BC, the gradient path, refusal).
2. The lever itself is fine: fed with the exact error of the critic, the same penalty wins back 94 of the 99 lost
   points in strong current (Stage 2's perfect band: 79.7 against −14.3 for no caution) and passed the weighting test's
   room check on expert and medium-expert data (+8.5 and +5.8 points).
3. With BCA's own band it does nothing, in every seed and both worlds: the band is flat across commands, so the penalty
   is 0.1-0.5% of the critic's over-optimism, 1/150 of the cloning term, the actor moves by 0.02 against 0.15 for the
   ramp, and the points equal no caution's (−13.0 against −13.2). The synthetic check says why: the lever's effect is
   the band's slope in the command, and that slope must be about 0.84 of the critic's to hold the actor at the knee;
   on the records it is 0.01.
4. Weighting the band through a classifier of the command makes the penalty large enough (effective k about 1 on
   training states) and the actor then recovers 39 of 99 points, but it penalizes harmless off-range commands just the
   same, is anti-correlated with harm under the shift, loses to a hand-set range limit by 46 points, and costs 15 points
   where full throttle is good. That is a range limit in disguise, not BCA's error band at work.
5. The consistent case is "flat band, no effect" (all 35 seed-settings); the exceptional cases are the handful of seeds
   where check (e)'s e4 flips and the three seeds where the band is narrower at the harmful command than at its cap.
