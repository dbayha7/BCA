After the stop: the data-only stand-ins are running

exploratory Outside the gate. No BCA: no band, calibrator, threshold or BCA update is fitted or used. Training started on {{beyond/status/standins_started_at#s}}; no result is reported here. Specification version {{beyond/status/standins/version#i}}.

The stand-ins test, a step of the plan for work beyond the data, asks whether simple penalties that look only at the recorded data can do what Stage 1's oracle did. Each of {{beyond/status/standins/n_penalties#i}} penalties is added to the actor's training, matched in strength to the oracle, and scored on seeds {{beyond/status/standins/seeds_lo#i}}–{{beyond/status/standins/seeds_hi#i}} in the stalling world and in the no-stall world. A penalty captures the room when, pooled over these seeds, its gain over no caution in strong current is at least {{beyond/status/standins/share_of_oracle#p0}} of the oracle's, it scores at least no caution minus {{beyond/status/standins/recorded_margin#i}} points in the recorded conditions, and it beats a matched even pull by at least {{beyond/status/standins/even_margin#i}} points in strong current.

Three penalties (density of the recorded commands, distance to the data, and disagreement between the critic's two heads) are measured relative to the row's own recorded command, a change from the plan's formulas; the plan's literal forms run beside them. The mark is carried by the other penalties and one form of each of these three. The user chose which form on {{beyond/status/standins_choice_at#s}}, before any mark was computed: the forms measured from the recorded command, with the plan's forms reported beside them. The choice was given in chat, in one message holding the specification's first 16 characters, accept chat mode (the user accepts that a chat message is weaker evidence than typing at a terminal) and {{beyond/status/standins_choice_words#s}} (the exact words the approval program accepts; decline F1-F3 would have chosen the plan's forms). The approval program refuses a second choice. All {{beyond/status/standins/n_penalties#i}} are trained, matched and scored the same way under either choice.

What changed in version {{beyond/status/standins/version#i}}. The user's choice is needed only by the marks look, the last step, which computes the marks. Training, strength matching, the lock and the evaluations do not wait for it. The choice must still come before the marks are seen, so the approval program refuses once the marks look has run. One limit stays: each seed's evaluation file holds the points of all {{beyond/status/standins/n_penalties#i}} penalties, so the choice is blind only if nobody works the marks out by hand first. The approval records which run files existed when it was written; none did. The two run lanes take their CPUs at launch, because all four CPUs are shared with other tests.
