===== BCA-X: BCA's band plus a stated prior on the critic's slope beyond the recorded range (ensemble and posterior routes ruled out by the evidence)
Angle A asked for a version of BCA's own machinery whose width grows where the critic has no data. On saved artifacts, none of the learned uncertainty available to BCA responds to the command. This covers: the gapped critic's two heads, two initialisations on the same data, a closed-form last-layer Bayesian posterior, and critics given a trace of the missing rows. For each, the change in width from the 80% cap to a harmful past-80% command is 0.5-0.9% of the false preference (0.63-0.89), so k would need to be about 110-190. The posterior's leverage is the same at the command and at the cap, and grows 9-13x only with state novelty (strong current). The step from 0.8 to 1.0 is a short, confident linear extrapolation, not variance. Disagreement that does see the harm exists only between the gapped critic and a critic trained on the removed commands, which is data the gapped log does not have.

So BCA's band can respond to the action only through a prior, and the design states that prior in BCA's own terms. BCA-X keeps the uniform band and adds an extrapolation term: U_X(s,a) = R*u_f*eta_f(s,a) + R_x * sum_k stopgrad|dQ1/da_k|(s, Pi(a)) * excess_k(a). Here Pi is the projection onto the recorded box (the hinge's own 0.5%/99.5% bounds) and R_x = 1. At k*R_x = 1 the lower edge gives no credit to any believed value gain beyond the recorded range. Past the box edge the penalty rises at exactly the rate of the critic's own pull. There is no hand-set scale and no classifier that extrapolates over states. The same object drives training, detection and deployment.

Honest expectation: about parity with the range hinge (M4 plausible) and a no-stall cost like the hinge's. It cannot beat the hinge's trade-off across the two worlds, because no signal calibrated on the data separates the worlds. Fallback: k = 2 as a declared secondary arm, plus the same width used only at deployment, which needs no training.

MECH: Why the learned routes fail. In the critic's own representation, a command 0.2 past the 80% cap is a short linear extrapolation along action directions the data already span. Leverage at the command equals leverage at the cap (1.4-1.7x the held-out rows). The two heads and the two initialisations therefore extrapolate alike and stay agreed, while the false preference comes from that shared extrapolation. The gapped log keeps only 34-59 past-80% rows. Doubling them moves the harmful preference by just 0.006-0.035, so reweighting the data with Bayesian-bootstrap masses cannot create the needed disagreement. What the data can calibrate cannot reach beyond the recorded range. The only honest statement BCA can make there is 'cannot certify', and turning that into a finite penalty requires a prior.

What BCA-X does. It states the prior in critic units: beyond the box, the critic's believed slope may be entirely wrong, so the width grows at |dQ1/da| per unit of excess. The lower edge Q1 - k*U_X then has slope (1 - k*R_x)*dQ1/da past the box. At k*R_x = 1 this cancels the pull, and at 2 it reverses it, at exactly the rate the critic pulls. The penalty is steep from the edge, which is what the M4 diagnosis said BCA lacked. It is state-independent in its support, so there is no classifier collapse at currents above 1.0 (the M5 failure). It needs no hand constant, unlike the hinge's HINGE_SCALE of 100. Inside the box it does nothing.

The same mechanism removes the truly good past-80% gains in the no-stall world. That is the cost of any caution based only on the data here.

===== BCA Certificate Guard, read on support (CSG), with a BCA-placed box as the fallback
Use BCA's weighted band through its certificate rather than its width, and act on it as a hard limit at run time. At every state the policy runs in, read the band at that state clamped to the training-state box (per-dimension 0.5%/99.5% quantiles), which is the support the band was calibrated on. Refuse any command that BCA's own weighted threshold cannot certify (classifier log w(s~,a) >= l*, which means R = +inf in the tabulated WBCP posterior), and clip a refused command to the recorded range. Certified commands pass unchanged. There is no soft penalty, and U~ and tau take no part in the decision.

Fallback (CSG-box): a constant per-thruster, per-sign limit, set at each refresh as the median of BCA's refusal boundary over the full-throttle-region training states. It is used as a hard clip at run time (and could serve as the hinge bounds in training).

Verdict, exploratory on seeds 0-4:
- **What the redesign fixes.** It makes BCA decisive and fixes M5's mechanics. The check beats random switching by +28.1, against +1.5 for the check as run.
- **What it does not fix.** Across the two worlds the gain is a hinge in disguise:
  - the guard nets the same as clipping always;
  - the box sits exactly on the symmetric-cap frontier within each world.
- **What is BCA's own share.** Only the placement of the refusal point l*. BCA's residual network contributes nothing measurable.

MECH: A conformal threshold covers the distribution it was calibrated on. BCA calibrates on all recorded rows pooled and then reads the band at the actor's own commands. The rows sitting where the actor goes (full throttle) are 0.1% of the pool and cannot move a pooled 90% quantile. The scale network, trained on batch-level coverage, never learned them either (eta is nearly flat). The weighted version tried to reach those commands with a classifier likelihood ratio. A classifier ratio cannot see residuals, so its threshold table depended on the test point only through the test-atom mass: a step to 'cannot certify', which is a novelty detector, and one that collapses at currents beyond its training range.

Stratifying the threshold by command magnitude makes the tail rows set the threshold for tail commands. Their TD residual carries the harm mechanism: past 80% the propeller stalls, the next state is worse, so y = r + gamma*min Q(s', c(s')) falls below the critic's prediction q. The overestimate is shifted by +0.17 to +0.30 in the stalling world and by about 0 in the no-stall world. BCA's WBCP posterior turns this into (a) a credible, world-level decision on whether the critic is worse past the edge and (b) a height in value units, the units of the critic's false preference. The command index makes the band read the same at any current, so it does not collapse at strong current. The certificate makes caution world-selective, which no data-only signal can be, because the in-range data are identical across worlds. Inside one world, the shape past the edge (a ramp across the empty 0.79-0.98 gap) is still a data-edge feature.

===== Outcome-reading BCA, tested on a two-world evidence sweep: held-out residuals, a command-local WBCP quantile, and Stage 2's own deployment rule
A range hinge and a novelty classifier only look at where the data lie. Of the three methods, BCA is the only one that reads outcomes (r, s') through its residuals. In ThrusterChain the two worlds have the same (state, command) data and differ only in the outcomes of a few full-throttle rows: 34-59 rows survive the gap, all at thruster 1 = -1.0. So reading outcomes is the one property that can beat the novelty trade-off, and BCA as built throws that evidence away in three places:
1. The scale network eta is fit on in-sample residuals of a critic trained on those same rows, so the trace fades.
2. R is a single global quantile over a bank with about 0 past-80% rows.
3. The weighted version's classifier weights measure joint novelty, so they cannot tell the actor's command from its clipped version at the same state.

Restructure:
- Use held-out (cross-fitted) one-step residuals as the conformity scores, with a one-sided overrating score.
- Take a WBCP weighted quantile with a test atom, localised on the command.
- Treat 'cannot certify' as the strongest caution.
- Consume it with Stage 2's own deployment rule.

Score every arm in both worlds together, along an evidence axis:
- S0: the gate's own gapped log with its survivors.
- Trace shares 0.5-10%: rows held out from the critic.

The rivals that cannot read outcomes are hinge/clip, classifier ratio, distance and BCA as built. References are the exact one-step oracle and random switching at the same rate.

Read-only result on seen seeds 0-4, net across worlds in strong current:
- Restructured band on the gate's own data: 97.9.
- Same rows with the outcomes shuffled: 10.9.
- Classifier ratio: 48.7. Clip every step: 44.2. BCA as built: 27.3.
- Exact oracle: 103.7.

So ThrusterChain CAN show BCA's distinct value. Because the stall depends only on the command, it favours command-only localisation. State-aware localisation and multi-critic aggregation need a mixed world (fallback test bed).
