== RECOMMENDATION
 The best place for BCA is not inside the host's conservatism term. It is at the decision to deviate from the clone: certify each deviation (or a declared group of deviations, or a whole-world switch) from held-out outcomes that contain the harm, let it through only when the WBCP bound clears a declared margin, and otherwise fall back to the clone (O8, score 6). This is the only placement whose promise is about the decision itself and that reads the certificate only where it was calibrated. Every ThrusterChain gain that avoided the no-stall cost came from reading held-out outcomes: step 1 world switch net 106.1, step 2 certificate-switched ramp 102.9 (ROUND1.md:23-39; step2_results/results.md:9-38; exploratory) [shown]. The training-side partner should be actor-side in value form, not cloning form: a lower edge Q1 - k*U_w at the actor's own smoothed command, recalibrated on Q1, with exact command weights, where a refusal means no value credit (O4, 4.5), switched on only where O8 certifies harm. Certified choice of alpha or checkpoint (O10, 4.0) is a cheap third, because that is where the return room actually is: a fixed stop at 5k updates gains +14.4/+10.4/+4.9 (placement/O10_checkpoint_room.out) [shown]. The honest caveat is that the more valid the placement, the less of BCA it uses. O8 and O10 keep only the WBCP sampler and weights, and with equal weights that is the exact binomial test (LIVE 12152). O4, the option that keeps the most BCA, has so far lost its credit to support rules, the classifier and the refusal rule: eta gave 0.2% of the extra width, and F - F0 = +0.14/+0.08 (LIVE 8020-8025, 9181-9188) [shown]. So BCA's own claim can only be earned in two places, its weights under a known shift (O8/O10) and the per-command shape of its band (O4), each against matched plain controls. Drop O0, O1, O3 and band-threshold deployment gates.

== ACTOR SIDE
 Yes, actor-side is better, but only in one form, and in that form BCA's band has not yet earned credit of its own. The current BC multiplier is itself actor-side (O0), and it ranks last. So the useful split is not actor vs critic. It is (a) where the band is read: the actor's own command or the deviation itself, not recorded training rows; and (b) what it acts on: value credit or an accept/reject decision, not the cloning strength.

The exact form I recommend has two parts.

(1) Decision layer (O8, policy extraction):
- Each deviation from the clone (or a declared deviation group, or a world-level switch) is accepted only if a WBCP bound on its held-out outcome clears a declared margin.
- The outcome must contain the harm: a k-step segment outcome against the critic's value at the fallback, not arm B's one-step critic-scored gain.
- Otherwise the policy falls back to the clone.

(2) Training-side consumer, switched on only where (1) certifies harm (O4 in pure form):
- The actor maximizes lambda*[Q1(s,a) - k*U_w(s,a)] - alpha*BC, at a = pi(s)+xi, xi ~ N(0,0.2) clipped at 0.5.
- U_w is recalibrated on Q1, the head TD3+BC's actor climbs. Every run so far used a min(Q1,Q2) band.
- Exact per-query state x command weights for that declared law.
- Unpaired edge with k = 1 (the paired [U(a)-U(a_i)]_+ is not calibrated).
- A refusal (+inf) means no value credit, with the clone as fallback, not the row's recorded command. That fallback was the -9.8 loss mechanism (O5 semantics).

This is the restructured step-2 pattern (certificate-switched ramp, net 102.9 vs 77.7 always on), with BCA's U_w taking the place of the hand-set shape.

Why this form: the room sits at the actor's command. The oracle value penalty there recovered 93.9 of 98.9 points, against ~2.6% through the pull channel (LIVE 7601-7622) [S]. It is also the only training-side read point where BCA's weighted guarantee can hold.

The caveats are shown, not guessed:
- In this slot, BCA's credit went to the classifier: 86% of the extra width vs 0.2% from eta (LIVE 8020-8025).
- It went to the refusal rule: F - F0 = +0.14/+0.08 against a +1.0 bar (LIVE 9181-9188).
- It lost to the plain range hinge: 24.9 vs 70.5.
- It costs 14.8-16.2 points in the no-stall world unless it is switched by outcomes.

The other actor-side form, band-gated imitation (O6), has a working slot (+10 to +19 over TD3+BC on weak data), but the band adds nothing over a plain margin of 1-2 residual spreads.

So: actor-side value or decision use, yes. A BCA-specific gain there is unproven until test T1's eta-share gate and test T5's controls (F0, ratio-only, eta shuffled, split conformal, plain cap) are passed.

== TRADEOFFS
  - Validity vs BCA-ness. The options whose promise holds where it is read (O8, O10, O9) keep only the WBCP sampler and weights, and with equal weights that is the exact binomial test (LIVE 12152). The option that keeps the whole band (O4) is where the guarantee is most strained (feedback shift, slope, head mismatch), and its measured credit went to support or the classifier [S].
  - Honest coverage by refusing vs return. With exact command weights, O4/O2 meet covered-or-refused by refusing 49-77% of draws, and 68-98% of commands on weak data (RESULTS_command_weighted_band.md). That turns into blanket caution where less caution helps (half pull +15.4/+8.7/+7.9). In O6, coverage-correct IW weighting cost 0.3-3.0 points [S].
  - Stalling-world gain vs no-stall cost. Every signal that sees only the data pays in the no-stall world: range hinge 20.4, BCA lower edge 14.8/16.2, distance check 25.7. Only switches that read outcomes escape it (net 106.1, 102.9), and plain tests with the same margin match them [S].
  - Privileged information. Result F's gain used the recorder's exact command likelihood plus a per-episode random-share label. With estimated support it held on expert (+2.3) and vanished on medium-expert, where it found only 5.0% of the needed refusals [S]. On D4RL every weight would be estimated.
  - Evidence size vs granularity. A tail certificate needs 29 tries per decision, about 39 under moderate shift and about 83 under strong shift (LIVE 10987-10992). ThrusterChain has 28-59 survivors per seed-world and 0-78 tries per 5% rung, so fine-grained per-deviation certificates mostly refuse, and the world-level switch is where the evidence suffices [S].
  - Prediction width vs confidence bound. BCA's band includes one-step outcome noise and does not shrink with data. That is right for 'is this outcome unusual' (O8 tails, O11 outliers), but too wide for comparing means (O6: margin 0 and 1-spread beat it by 2.5-3.1) and the wrong object for pessimism (O1/O2) [S/I].
  - Margin vs calibration. The hand-set 0.10 margin carried about 60% of BCA's lead in step 1; without it BCA netted 55.7 instead of 106.1 (ROUND1.md:34, 108-119). Any certificate placement has to fix its margin in advance, and the margin, not the posterior, may be doing the work [S].
  - Decision-side vs training-side. A frozen, held-out certificate keeps its promise. A band inside training is rescored by critics it shaped (198 refreshes), is frozen for up to 5,000 updates, and is queried at points the actor chooses, so the promise holds only vacuously or formally fails [S/I].
  - Deterministic actors. TD3+BC/ReBRAC actors have no density ratio, so O4, O9 and O10 must declare a smoothing law, and the certificate then covers a different, noisier policy. Near the 80% knee the noise itself causes stalls [I].
  - Global knob vs per-sample caution. The return room on weak data sits in the dataset-level pull strength and in early stopping (O10). BCA was designed to replace that global knob with a per-sample factor, so the placement with the most room is the one least like BCA's stated purpose [S/I].

== MISSING OPTIONS
  - O8b, a harm certificate on a declared group or world. Default: leave the host alone. A plain caution action (clip or range hinge) switches on only when the group's held-out one-sided residuals credibly exceed the rest by a margin fixed in advance. This is what step 1 and step 2 actually ran (ROUND1.md:11), the opposite direction from O8's safety certificate. It holds the only shown ThrusterChain gain (net 106.1, 10/10 seed-worlds right), and plain two-sample tests with the same margin match it (ROUND1.md:31-34) [S].
  - Margin calibration: derive the effect size the certificate tests against from the decision's break-even, instead of setting it by hand. The 0.10 margin was picked after seeing seeds 0-2 (step1/PRESPEC.md:156-168). It carried the false-alarm control (55.7 without it, ROUND1.md:34) and landed near break-even by luck (ROUND1.md:90) [S].
  - The fallback action as a declared variable (clip to the recorded range, one rung down, or the clone). The shown gains depend on it more than on the trigger. With a perfect detector: clip +106.1 against clone +73.8 in strong current, and +28.2 against -30.4 in big kicks (HV2 results-2026-10-13/deploy_tables.md) [S].
  - Certificate-guarded release of the host's conservatism (arm C: half pull guarded by a certified throttle ceiling). It is the only placement aimed at where the weak-data room lies (+15.5/+8.9/+8.5, LIVE 10044-10059). Credit went to half pull and a plain 80% cap; the guard was void in 18 of 25 pairs [S].
  - A conformalised epistemic or support score at the actor's command, UNIQ-style: BCA's R sets the level, and an ensemble, classifier or density sets the shape. It appears only as an open question. ThrusterChain's priors are weak: two-head disagreement 1.2/4.0, a last-layer posterior varies by at most 0.0008, classifier 36.8/11.0 (LIVE 7986-7993, 8218-8221) [S].
  - An online randomised certificate before deployment. Flip a coin per episode to turn the gate on or off, and certify the gate-on minus gate-off return with an anytime-valid mean gate. It is the only form whose outcome is the decision's own return. It exists only as arm A4 in O11's test, not as a placement in the table [I].
  - Report-only critic audit: publish per-group held-out residual certificates as a trust report, with no consumer. The promise is valid where it is read, and there is no return claim. It is the honest fallback if every consumer test fails [I].
  - A two-sided dose that may go below 1. Closed by algebra: calibration fixes the mean dose and the saturating map squeezes out the per-row spread (rootcause mech), so it reduces to a global retune of alpha, where the half-pull gain already sits [SS].

== REVISED RECOMMENDATION
 Revised recommendation (19 Oct 2026). Read-only; nothing was trained or run. Every ThrusterChain number is exploratory, on seeds 0-4 already seen, unless marked otherwise. Tags: [S] shown, [SS] strongly supported, [I] inferred. Number checks are in /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-BCA/8720079f-929e-55d9-81af-561745cb590b/scratchpad/placement/redteam_checks.txt.

Terms:
- Harm certificate: switch caution on only when held-out evidence credibly shows harm; otherwise leave the host alone.
- Safety certificate: allow a deviation only when held-out evidence credibly shows it is fine; otherwise fall back.
- Residual: how far the critic's one-step prediction overshot what the step delivered, scored by a critic that did not train on that step.
- Margin: the effect size, fixed in advance, that the evidence must clear.

1. The owner is right about the multiplier. Drop the dose on TD3+BC's cloning term (O0). It amounts to retuning alpha from 2.5 to about 1.88. It cannot go below 1, where less pull helps (half pull +15.4/+8.7/+7.9, LIVE 10046-10051), and it loses 5-9 points on weak data (MAIN outputs/shift_iw/explore/RESULTS.md:103-107) [S]. Also drop the pessimistic critic target (O1), band-weighted TD loss (O3) and deployment gates that threshold the band (O11).

2. Best place on the evidence: a harm certificate on a declared group of deviations, or on a whole world, that switches a plain caution action (O8b). The ranking calls this O8, but O8 as it wrote it is a safety certificate. The shown gains come from the harm direction, and with ThrusterChain's evidence the two do not behave alike.
- How it works: read BCA's own score, the one-sided residual, on the declared group (full-throttle steps) against all other steps, using rows the critic did not train on. Switch on the clip to the recorded range, or a gated range hinge, only when the 5% lower bound of the gap clears the margin. Otherwise leave the host alone.
- What it showed: net 106.1, equal to a perfect switch; right in 10 of 10 seed-worlds (ROUND1.md:31-32). The gated hand-set hinge gives 107.4 (ROUND1.md:155) [S].
- What is BCA's own: only the residual score as the outcome (ANSWER.md:19) [S]. The band, eta and the dose take no part. The posterior beat split conformal by just 1.78 against a 5-point bar. A plain difference of means with the same margin also nets 106.1, and without the margin BCA nets 55.7 (ROUND1.md:34, 109) [S].
- What its promise covers: one-step residuals of a group in recorded conditions. It does not cover the decision. The link to return rests on a hand-set margin, picked after seeing seeds 0-2. In-sample survivors also reached 106, so reading held-out rows was not shown to matter (ROUND1.md:39) [S].
- Theory: reasonable; not Seldonian.

3. Theory-natural but unshown: a safety certificate on each deviation (O8a, the Seldonian/SPIBB direction).
- This is the only place BCA's weights could earn credit: in the toys they said yes 33.6% of the time in safe shifted worlds, against 19.0% for rejection sampling (LIVE 10811) [S].
- But under weights the promise is empirical, not proven at these sizes (MAIN experiments/wbcp/DEPENDENCE.md:1114-1133) [S]. Estimated weights are untested in the toys.
- On ThrusterChain, held-out evidence is 15-30 tries per seed-world, at or below the 29-try floor (ROUND1.md:25; LIVE 10516) [S]. It would mostly refuse and turn into clip-always, net 44.4 [I].
- Its fallback must be the clip, not the clone. With a perfect detector, the clone fallback scores +73.8 against +106.1 in strong current, and -30.4 against +28.2 in big kicks (deploy_tables.md) [S].

4. Hygiene, not a place for BCA: give refusal (+inf) one meaning in every host, 'no value credit, fall back to the clip' (O5). Credit it to support (wbcp.py:43-54; F - F0 = +0.14/+0.08) [S].
