== ROOT CAUSE
 BCA takes its caution from calibrated one-step TD errors at the recorded (state, command) rows. It applies that caution as a bounded multiplier, between 1 and 1.5, on the host's own conservatism term at those same rows. The host actually fails somewhere else: its critic over-values the actor's own commands off the data (past 80% throttle), so both BCA's signal and its lever sit where the error is not. Either half alone is enough to block a gain. Exact harm information fed through BCA's lever recovers 2.6% of the room (H3: -1.9 to +1.1 against +3). BCA's own band fed through the right lever recovers about 0. Only exact information acting on the value at the actor's command recovers the loss, 93.9 of 98.9 points. What remains of BCA is a near-uniform dose of about 1.33, which is just the host with its coefficient retuned (TD3+BC alpha 2.5 to about 1.88): it costs 5.3-8.8 points on weak data and ties on good data. All of this is shown for TD3+BC on ThrusterChain; for the other hosts it is inferred from the code.

== CHAIN
  1 1. DESIGN PREMISE (shown, as quoted). The repository's own literature review states the premise: 'the Bellman residual is a natural place to read per-sample uncertainty', and 'treating conservatism as a dose to be set per sample rather than per dataset is the natural next step' (docs/literature_review LIT 1304, 1308). Both of BCA's halves follow from it.
  2 2. SIGNAL ONLY AT RECORDED ROWS (shown, code). The error-size network is fit on training batches, the threshold on bank rows taken after the removal (851 survive), and the dose is read at batch.obs and batch.action (origin/main:algorithms/td3_bc_bca.py:170-174, 252-257, 283-285; experiments/shift/learners.py:207-208, 257; live_c264.txt:2323). No residual is ever computed at pi(s) or in the removed full-throttle region. Every host reads the band at the recorded (s,a) (unifloral_*_bca.py; ../unifloral/notes/result.json).
  3 3. THAT RESIDUAL CANNOT SEE THE HARMFUL ERROR (shown). ThrusterChain is deterministic (experiments/shift/thruster_chain.py:13), so a recorded-row residual holds only fit error, target noise and lag. Bias that already satisfies the Bellman equation cancels in it: the LQ harness misses such a bias on 45-100% of rows while coverage stays near 91% (origin/main:experiments/signal/SIGNAL_STUDY.md:74-77). The critic fits the full-throttle rows it trains on: in-sample error -0.24/-0.06/+0.10, against -0.49/-0.50 for a critic that never saw them (live 8212). Only 0-2 of the surviving full-throttle rows reach the bank (live 8099-8101). Where BCA reads, the gapped critic is accurate: error 0.07, against 0.12 for the full-log critic (live 7458-7462).
  4 4. SO THE BAND MEASURES WHERE THE DATA LIE AND THE NOISE THERE, NOT THE ERROR (shown). Rank correlation with critic error is 0.10-0.33, against 0.52-0.76 with a noise-only map (main session 16 Oct 15:38:54 UTC, item 6). The width is 0.31-0.36 at a harmful past-80% command, at the 80% cap and at the recorded command, differing by at most 0.008, while the false preference it would have to cancel is 0.63-0.89 (live 8089-8095). Published detection size 0.0004 (live 8006). Coverage of the actor's commands past 80% is 48.4% (medium-expert) and 24.7% (expert) (live 9006-9009). The page's own words: 'BCA's band measures where the data lie, not what happens there' (live 8095).
  5 5. CALIBRATION PINS THE DOSE NEAR 1.33 ON EVERY ROW (shown, algebra and measurement). With m = 1 + 0.5 z/(1+z) and z = R*eta, the unit cancels (calibration/dose.py:53-60, 74-76; dose_algebra_check.out section 1). Calibration puts z near the 90% point of a unit-RMS score, 1.56-1.65 and at most 3.16, so m is 1.31-1.38 for any residual shape (sections 3-4). Doubling eta moves m by only +0.07 (section 5). The flat eta of step 4 compresses the spread further: 0.106 against 0.239 for an ideal conditional band (section 7; stylized model, inferred). Measured: dose 1.32-1.34, 99% even (live 9961-9963). Page row 209 recomputes exactly: 1.3282 (live_c264.txt:2810, 2887).
  6 6. THE LEVER IS ALWAYS MORE OF THE HOST'S OWN AT-DATA CONSERVATISM (shown, code). In TD3+BC the dose multiplies the cloning term (algorithms/td3_bc.py:128-139). In CQL it multiplies the per-state gap with weight 10, in ReBRAC actor_bc_coef 0.001 (unifloral_rebrac_bca.py:195-199), and in IQL it shrinks the A>0 weights toward 1 (calibration/advantage.py:32-70). It never penalizes Q at pi(s), and in TD3+BC and ReBRAC it never touches the critic. It shrinks the actor's step away from the data by at most a third (mechanism_notes.txt section 3), and the dose cannot fall below 1. The pull points toward a recorded thruster-1 throttle of 0.51, while the actor's averages 0.71, so 'a harder pull says drive like the recording, not stop at 80%' (live 7462-7464).
  7 7. THE HARM IS A FALSE PREFERENCE AT THE ACTOR'S OWN OFF-DATA COMMANDS (shown). Past 80% the critic's value rises +1.1 per unit of thruster-1 throttle where the truth falls 3.9. == CHANGE
  - 1. Move the lever onto the value at the actor's own command: a lower edge on Q(s, pi(s)), or no value credit for commands the data lack, instead of a multiplier on the pull or gap at recorded rows. This addresses R1 (Stage 1: +93.9; F0). Still BCA? Only partly. BCA's band can be read at pi(s) (Stage 2's lower edge), but the defining 'dose on the host's own conservatism' is gone. On its own, the change gets about 0 from BCA's error model (90.6 against 90.7), so it must be paired with change 2.
  - 2. Give the signal information off the data: held-out outcomes of the surviving rows near the actor's commands (these separate the worlds: 0.78-1.05 against -0.05 to +0.02, live 8119-8121), residuals along the policy's own path (the true error is (I - gamma P^pi)^-1 applied to minus the TD residual, which no data row measures), or a support/novelty term. This addresses R2. Still BCA? Mostly not. The restructured BCA did this and won (106.1/102.9), but the credit went to reading held-out outcomes against a fixed 0.10 margin: split conformal 97.3 with the same decisions 10/10, rank test 9/10, 55.7 without the margin. BCA's posterior is then one interchangeable valid test. Its thin-evidence edge (+1.78/+4.29 against a bar of 5) was not met.
  - 3. Make the dose two-sided (allowed below 1), not pinned by calibration, and sized per dataset. This fixes the weak-data sign (half pull +15.4/+8.7/+7.9) but is tuning the host's coefficient, which the literature already does over 4x-2000x ranges. It is not BCA's per-sample idea, and it does not reach the stall harm (even x3 recovers 17 of 99 points at a cost of 5-13 elsewhere).
  - 4. Cosmetic fixes: a pinball (quantile) loss for eta, Q1-aligned scores, 'refusal means most caution', different refresh timing. These are still BCA, but they are predicted not to change the null: an ideal conditional band gives a dose spread of at most 0.239 (stylized), perfect placement in this channel is worth about 1 point (H3), and Q1 alignment moves the dose by at most 0.011.
  - 5. Host-specific rewiring: in CQL, put the dose per action inside the logsumexp; in ReBRAC, dose critic_bc_coef rather than the 0.001 actor coefficient; in IQL, compare a unit-free width with the advantage. These are still BCA-shaped and untested. The CQL and ReBRAC-critic versions would put the lever on the value side, and so separate R1 from R2 in those hosts.
  - Overall: any change that addresses both root halves replaces both BCA's defining signal and its defining lever. The part that would stay specific to BCA, the Bayesian quantile posterior, equals the exact binomial test (28,000/28,000). The result would therefore not be BCA in its present sense, but a support- or outcome-aware value penalty or gate that can use a conformal test as a component.

== REVISED ROOT CAUSE
 BCA as built cannot improve its host because of its lever, and on ThrusterChain also because of its signal.

(1) Lever. This is the general half, shown for TD3+BC on ThrusterChain and in the LQ harness. BCA's only action is a per-row scalar multiplier, between 1 and 1.5, on the host's own conservatism term at recorded (state, command) rows. Calibration pins its mean near 1.31-1.38 whatever the signal. The saturating map then compresses row-to-row differences to about 0.11 of dose per ln-unit of the band. So the multiplier works as a one-sided strength knob (TD3+BC alpha 2.5 becomes about 1.88), not as targeting. Even exact harm information placed in this lever is worth about 1 point in the shifted situations. In strong current it is negative: H3 harm-placed minus strength-matched even was -1.9, 5/5 seeds. Pull placed by the exact error still loses 37-58 return units on weak data. The measured outcome follows from this: -5.3 to -8.8 points on weak data, where the host needs less pull (half the pull: +15.4/+8.7/+7.9), and a tie on good data, where the pull channel's room is 2.6% of the value channel's.

(2) Signal. This half is specific to ThrusterChain and partly built into the testbed. The harm is a false preference for the actor's own commands past 80%, carried into strong-current states outside the data. BCA's band is fitted in-sample, by a marginal-coverage objective, on residual sizes at recorded rows. In this deterministic world those residuals mostly track TD3's target-smoothing noise: Spearman 0.52-0.76 with the noise map against 0.10-0.33 with the critic's error. Off the data the band extrapolates flat: its widths at harmful, capped and recorded commands differ by at most 0.008, against a false preference of 0.63-0.89; detection size was 0.0004 and direction 50% at training states. So it carries neither support nor outcome information where the harm is.

Everything that did reach the harm acted on the value at the actor's own command, using support or held-out outcome information:
- the exact oracle: +93.9;
- the range hinge or clip: +84.6 to +107.4, costing 20-31 points in the no-stall world;
