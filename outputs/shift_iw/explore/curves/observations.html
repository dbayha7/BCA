All 330 reruns that have an earlier run end at its saved actor bit for bit. The other 75 are the new arms on the three lower qualities, with nothing to match. Version 5's runs reproduce only when each is pinned to one processor core, as they were first trained, so they were rerun that way.
What the curves show
On expert data, training the actor against the critic costs return, and the band weighted for the actor's commands prevents that. The actor fitted to the recorded commands already scores 95.2 points on the strong test. Trained with no extra caution it falls to 91.1 within 5,000 steps and ends at 90.7, as the critic draws it past 80%. BCA's band as version 5 used it and the untargeted control follow the same path. Weighted for the actor's own commands, the actor instead rises to 96.5; the perfect lower edge reaches 99.1. Estimated weights stop part of the fall (93.0).
On medium-expert the first test's collapse is visible as it happens. Every actor-only run climbs from 51.9 points within its first 1,000 to 3,000 steps. The first test, which gave no value credit at every refusal, peaks at 92.8 after 3,000 steps and then falls to 82.3, as its refusals become copies of random recorded commands. The fixed fallback stays near its peak: 94.8 after 3,000 steps, 94.1 at the end.
On the three lower qualities the actor-only curves nearly coincide. They peak after 5,000 to 16,000 steps and end 1.5 to 3 points lower. The weighted arms stay within 1 point of the actor with no extra caution throughout.
Full TD3+BC training peaks early on the lower qualities. Plain TD3+BC, with or without the removal, scores best after 5,000 to 15,000 updates and then loses 4 to 17 points by 100,000 updates; on random data, after the removal, the strong test falls from 80.6 to 66.0. BCA's capped pull peaks after 10,000 updates and loses more, 9 to 23 points. On medium-expert and expert the first checkpoint, 5,000 updates, is already about the best, and later training costs 0.3 to 2.2 points. Every result of this test is read at the last checkpoint, as offline training must be: the test return that could pick an earlier one is not available while training.
Expected against what happened: the new arms on the three lower qualities
Written before these runs: the weighted arms within ±10 units of return of version 5's unweighted lower edge on the strong test, the untargeted controls within ±5, and every curve close to that of the actor with no extra caution. Mostly met. Exact weights: +5.9 (random), −4.6 (medium-replay), −8.4 (medium). Estimated weights: +5.8 and −8.2, and −11.4 on medium, just outside. Untargeted controls: −3.2 to −6.1, three of the six beyond 5. The curves track that of the no-caution actor within 1 point. On these qualities every episode holds random commands, so the weighting refuses the actor's commands only for want of calibration rows and gives them the capped threshold: a mild caution that costs a few units, as the controls' do.
